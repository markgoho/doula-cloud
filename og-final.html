Finishing the line

You keep the first one. says what she keeps and never says who takes the rest — so the product does no work in the sentence, and the reader is left holding half a thought. Four ways to close it.

One constraint worth keeping in view: the product opens in January 2027, so a present-tense claim like DoulaCloud handles your admin would describe something that does not exist yet. All four below stay on the right side of that.

1. “DoulaCloud takes the other three.” Recommended

Names the product and counts the strikes, so the line and the picture say the same thing. Concrete: the rest is vague, the other three is what she can see.

Doula.
Scheduler.
Bookkeeper.
Invoice chaser.
DoulaCloud takes the other three.

2. “Let DoulaCloud handle the rest.” Yours

Your line. Clear and complete. Handle the rest is the phrasing every software company uses, which is the only thing against it.

3. “You keep the first one. I’ll take the other three.” Matches the page voice

Two beats, and it matches the page — the letter is written in the first person by one man, and this sounds like him. Warmest, and the longest.
