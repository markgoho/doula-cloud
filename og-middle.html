The middle word

You are right that Bookkeeper. and Invoice chaser. overlap. Two of the three struck roles are money, so the list shows two domains where it could show three — and the product looks narrower than it is.

They are not identical: bookkeeping is recording what happened, invoice chasing is asking a woman four days postpartum for money. But Invoice chaser. already carries the money domain, and carries it far more sharply, so it is the one to keep.
