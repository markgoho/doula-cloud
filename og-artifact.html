The strike: straight, tilted, thinner

A straight rule at a slight angle, a different angle on every line — one down, one up, one down — so it reads as three separate pen strokes rather than a rendered effect. Thinner too, so the word underneath survives.

One thing the angle costs: rotation happens about the middle of the word, so the ends rise and fall. Invoice chaser. is nearly twice the width of Scheduler., which means the same angle lifts its ends roughly twice as far. Past about 8 degrees the long line starts leaving the word behind — which is why there is a steeper option below to judge rather than a promise that it works.

1. Coral, 6px, 5–7° Recommended

Thin enough to read every struck word, angled enough to look drawn rather than applied.
