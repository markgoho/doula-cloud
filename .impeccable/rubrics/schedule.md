# Schedule: what a 4 means

A heuristic scores 4 only when **every** check under it passes, each with evidence a grader can point to: a screenshot, a measured value, the accessible text, or a walk's act log. A check with no evidence fails. 3 = one check fails, 2 = two, 1 = three or more, 0 = the heuristic's core job is absent.

Graders are three independent agents per round. None sees an earlier score, this branch's commits, or another grader's output. The score per heuristic is the median of the three.

Seed: the demo Practice under `dev:full` (Rooted Birth Collective, 14 doulas, 30+ Clients, 40 or more scheduled Visits in the next 30 days across the month, some earlier in the last week, two booked Visits whose Doula was taken off the Engagement, one of them past the first page, one Doula with two Visits on one day). Widths: 1440px, 1280px, 768px, 375px; 200% zoom at 1280px.

## Heuristic checks

1. **Visibility of system status:** loading shows a skeleton within 300ms and nothing moves when the rows land. Every filter change visibly changes the list or says that nothing matched. The count line states how many Visits are shown and, while more exist, how many there are in all, the days, the Doula and the Client's name when narrowed, and the order chosen. Load more states progress.
2. **Match with the real world:** every label is a word a doula or an office manager uses. Every day and time reads as ADR-0022's 2026-10-24 amendment writes a Visit's ("Thu Sep 4, 2:00pm"), with the year only when it is not this one. Days and times are the Practice's own (ADR-0036), and where the reader's zone is another the screen says which zone they are in. A Doula who is no longer on the Engagement is said in the words the Engagement page says it in (`NO_LONGER_ON_THIS_ENGAGEMENT`, `app/src/lib/engagementDetail.ts`; #2660).
3. **User control and freedom:** every filter is in the address (back, reload and share keep it), a stepped window and the order included; the Client search alone is kept out of the address (#1829) and is kept by Back, as on the Invoices list. Any filter can be cleared in one action, and one action returns to the days the screen opens on. Leaving for an Engagement and coming back restores the filter and position.
4. **Consistency and standards:** title, intro, filter bar, result line and table, the pieces this screen shares with the Clients list and the Invoices list, are the same components in the same place (measured: same title x/size, same filter bar arrangement, same result line, same table header and row style). Applied filters are tags as on those lists. A Visit's day and time read the same here as on its Engagement, by one shared formatter. Badge colors and words mean what they mean on the other lists. A convention the category has taught this screen's readers (the surface brief's Category baseline) is on the screen, or the brief records its decline with a reason that holds for a reader those products have trained; a convention a grader names that the baseline omits fails until the baseline records it.
5. **Error prevention:** a range that can never match (To before From) is not sent, or is answered in place beside the field, never by a full error page. A reader is offered only the narrowing the BFF will serve them: a contractor Doula sees only Visits on Engagements she holds a granted Attachment to, and no control lists the roster to a reader the BFF refuses it. Nothing on the list changes a Visit in one press.
6. **Recognition rather than recall:** the order is stated, serves the job, and is the reader's to choose (soonest or latest first). At 42rem of bar and wider filters are visible, not hidden in menus; below it the fold's summary says what it holds and an applied narrowing inside it shows as a tag. A named day's Visits are found without working a weekday out from a date: the Visits of each day sit under one heading that names the day with its weekday ("Today, Fri Nov 13", "Sun Nov 15"), one heading per day however many pages are loaded, and every row's day still starts with its weekday, at one x in every row. Each row says, without opening it, the Client, the day and time, and who holds it; a row that needs someone to act (its Doula is off the Engagement) carries the warning Badge beside its words, so it is found by scanning the column.
7. **Flexibility and efficiency:** T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T9 and T10 each pass within their action budgets. On the seed, the next seven days' Visits are all on page one, or one narrowing shows them with no Load more.
8. **Aesthetic and minimalist design:** amber only on the warning Badge of a row that needs someone to act, never a row or cell tint (DESIGN.md, One Meaning Per Hue); no red. Table rows have equal rhythm (row heights within 4px of each other); record cards keep one field order. No element on the page carries no information, no fact appears twice within one table row, and the order is stated once on the page. At 1280×800 and at 375×740, row 1 (Client, day and time, Doula) is wholly on the first screen, under the title and the filters.
9. **Error recovery:** T8 passes. A filter with no results says so and offers the way back to the days the screen opens on.
10. **Help and documentation:** every filter's effect is clear from its label, including whether the To day is included. The screen says which days it shows when no one has chosen any. The empty state for each filter says what was looked for and what to do. No term needs a glossary.

## Cross-cutting (each failure costs a point from the heuristic named)

- 320px and 200% zoom: no horizontal page scroll, every control usable (4, 7).
- WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, focus not obscured, target size 24px (4, 7).
- The constraints hold: ADR-0021 GOV.UK patterns, ADR-0022 dates carry the exact instant in a `<time>`, ADR-0024 intrinsic layout (no viewport queries), the Voice rules (4, 2).

## Changes to this rubric

- 2026-11-13, after the Schedule's climb closed: H4 gained the category baseline check. The jobs came from the persona records and no check asked what a screen of this kind has taught its readers to expect, so three rounds scored the Schedule 40/40 without anyone asking whether its table should sort (#2866). The check was missing a part of the reader's job, which is the ground for changing a rubric. The same day the check was sharpened: a recorded decline passes only when its reason holds for a reader the category has trained, since the first baseline's declines of a sort were recorded and wrong (#2869).
- 2026-11-13, #2867: H6 gained the day heading. The category baseline (#2866) found that a schedule is read a day at a time and the screen made a reader find a day's end by reading every row; the check was missing part of the reader's job.
- 2026-11-13, #2867: H2 holds the Practice's zone. ADR-0036 reads a Practice's calendar days in its own zone, never the reader's, and the screen used the browser's; the category baseline's two readings found it (#2866).
- 2026-11-13, #2867: H1 asks for the total. The category baseline (#2866) found that the Invoices list says "the first 30 of 35" and this list said no total; the endpoint lacking the figure was a reason to add it, not to leave it out.
- 2026-11-13, #2870: T6 reads the type. Priya's blind walk asked "is it a prenatal or the birth?" and the Engagement already showed it; the category baseline's graders named it.
- 2026-11-13, #2869/#2863: T7 no longer counts stops. The keyboard reader's job is to skip the bar, which "Skip to results" does, and to operate every control; six was the bar's size when the rubric was written, not a job, and a bar that holds what the category baseline asks for cannot keep it.
- 2026-11-13, #2870/#2863/#2869: T9 and T10 are new, T3 asks for the narrowing, H1, H3, H6 and H7 follow. The category baseline (#2866) found that a schedule's readers search by person, step through time, choose the order and narrow to what needs them, and four blind walks as Dee read every row for want of the narrowing; the jobs were missing from the rubric, which is the ground for changing it. H6's "filters are visible" now allows the phone fold: an always-drawn bar cost a phone reader the first card (#2869, the bar's design call).
