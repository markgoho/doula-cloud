## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed at the craft level of Linear, Stripe Dashboard and GOV.UK services (ADR-0063): the familiar arrangement with nothing out of place. The Contracts list is the Invoices list's sibling and reads as the same product: same title, filter bar, result line and table, with agreements in place of money.

OWN-WORLD: The Staff world as `DESIGN.md` records it. One blue for links and the primary action. A badge only where a state is unfinished, in `DESIGN.md`'s hues: amber where the Practice must act (a Contract not sent yet, a void requested), blue where it waits on the Client (sent and not signed). Signed and voided are plain words on a list, and red is on nothing. A day is said the way a person says it, and a wait as a count of days; tabular figures for both.

STORY: An office manager opens Contracts and reads, before reading a row, how many Contracts are not signed and which has waited longest. The Contract that was made and never sent is told apart from the one the Client is sitting on, because the next step differs: send it, or chase her. A void a Doula asked for is waiting at the top for the person who can decide it. An owner finds any Client's Contract, signed or not, without opening a filing cabinet or asking anyone. A Doula sees the Contracts of the births she is on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the nav; title "Contracts"; the void requests waiting for a decision, when there are any and the reader may decide them; the filter bar above the result line; the list starting in the first screen at 1280px and 375px.

FORM: The category standard (canon). No signature component: a list is not a record (ADR-0063).

## Where this screen differs from the climbed lists, and why

- **Two work lists, one address.** The Contracts, and the void requests nobody has decided (#971: "sees the void requests waiting on them" beside the Contracts, not a second address). The void requests are a short queue, never paged in practice, read by an Owner or Admin only (`mount.go`); the Contracts are the list the bar filters.
- **What a reader holds differs by role in the BFF, and the screen follows it.** An employed Doula reads every Contract at the Practice and a contractor only those on an Engagement she holds a granted attachment on (ADR-0008's Engagements row; #973). Neither decides a void, so neither is shown the void requests, and neither meets that read's refusal (#2851).
- **The amount is on the row for a reader who may read it.** An Owner, an Admin and an employed Doula read Contract money; a contractor who holds neither role reads the list without it (ADR-0008's Money line).
- **A Contract has no expiry and no reminder.** The lifecycle is draft, sent, signed, voided (`GLOSSARY.md:59`); nothing lapses.
- **The default view is what is not signed,** not the whole book: the screen was made for chasing (#273, DW-G5). The whole book is one choice away.
