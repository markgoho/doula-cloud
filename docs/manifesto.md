# Manifesto

One line per value the founder decided under [ADR-0041](adr/0041-a-book-enters-the-repo-as-evidence-and-becomes-a-rule-only-through-a-decision.md), and nothing else; the ADR holds the reasons, the evidence weighed, and what was rejected.

- **Doula Cloud is paid when the doula is.** A Practice pays one flat price per Engagement and nothing in between, every feature included, and a stranger runs three families through the product before any card is taken. [ADR-0042](adr/0042-a-practice-pays-per-engagement-and-doula-cloud-is-paid-when-the-doula-is.md)
- **A date is a trigger only when someone set it.** A message goes out on a date because a person's own act or the law fixed that date in advance, never because time passed in silence, and what a Client hears and when is her Practice's to set. [ADR-0043](adr/0043-a-date-is-a-trigger-only-when-someone-set-it.md)
- **The founder goes where doulas already gather, and the site is arrived at.** He speaks there as a person, the money buys presence and never attention, and a doula reaches the January site from a link, a post, or a person who said its name. [ADR-0044](adr/0044-the-founder-goes-where-doulas-already-gather-and-the-site-is-arrived-at.md)
- **A doula is heard before she is chosen.** The founder knows how the work is done, and nothing about what she pays for, which of the two segments buys, or the words she types is claimed until a doula has said it in a call or done it on the product; the nine personas stay hypotheses, and nobody is a customer until she has bought a Credit. [ADR-0045](adr/0045-a-doula-is-heard-before-she-is-chosen.md)
- **The product counts what a Practice did, and never who she is.** A Credit bought and a Credit spent are the two acts the product reads, a visitor is a number on the marketing site and nobody inside the app, and the founder reads a page once a month on which no figure is a goal. [ADR-0046](adr/0046-the-product-counts-what-a-practice-did-and-never-who-she-is.md)
- **A doula sees what moves her, and her own words do the moving.** A technique is open only if the founder would say, on the same surface, what it is and why it is there, and she would still be glad she acted; the site states facts in verbs, its stakes are what a doula said she lost, a testimonial is her words asked once after a spent Credit, and no money, Credit or title ever rides on a recommendation. [ADR-0047](adr/0047-a-doula-sees-what-moves-her-and-her-own-words-do-the-moving.md)
- **Nothing holds a Practice here but the next family.** The product refuses to make leaving cost more than staying, she leaves at any time with everything she put in, and it keeps her only by being worth a Credit the next time a family comes; what a competitor cannot copy without unmaking itself is the model and the refusals that follow from it, and the feature list follows from the Engagement and from nothing else. [ADR-0048](adr/0048-nothing-holds-a-practice-here-but-the-next-family.md)
