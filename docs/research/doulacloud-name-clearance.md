# Is DoulaCloud clear to use: trademark, domains, and handles

Research for [issue #996](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/996), on the map [Go to market (#991)](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/991). Question: **is "DoulaCloud" (and "Doula Cloud") clear to use as a product name in the United States: trademark, domains, and social handles?**

Facts held fixed, from the map: the legal entity is **Briandor LLC**, New York; the product trades as **"Doula Cloud"** today and the owner leans toward **"DoulaCloud"**; **doula.cloud** is already owned. The classes that matter are the ones this product would file in: **9** (downloadable software), **35** (business services, directories), **42** (software as a service), and **44** (medical and health services, where doula services usually sit).

**This is not legal advice.** It is a record of what the registers and platforms themselves say, with each claim traced to the page that owns it. Researched **8 September 2026**; every "free", "held", or "unverified" line below is as of that date, and handles and domains in particular can change the next day.

Sources are first-party throughout: the USPTO's own search service and fee schedule, the New York Department of State's entity database and its open-data mirror, the registries' RDAP and WHOIS servers, the platforms' own profile pages, and our own Squarespace, Firebase, and YouTube accounts. Where a platform blocked the read, the item is marked unverified rather than guessed.

## The findings that matter

1. **No trademark blocks the name.** The USPTO's search index holds no live or dead application or registration for `DOULACLOUD` or `DOULA CLOUD` in any class. The word DOULA is crowded (293 marks contain it, 149 live), and there are live software marks near ours (DIGITAL DOULA, DOULALAH, DOULA DOC & ME, DOULAREADI), but none pairs DOULA with CLOUD and none is a bar to filing. The closest sound-alikes, DOOLA (Reg. 7177039, class 42, business-formation SaaS) and DULA (abandoned 2025), are outside the doula field.
2. **The names we need are ours or free.** doula.cloud and doulacloud.com are both in the markgoho@gmail.com Squarespace Domains account. doulacloud.co, .io, .us, .net, .org, .ai and the obvious typos are unregistered. doulacloud.app is the one domain a third party holds, parked at GoDaddy since July 2024.
3. **The handle is a mixed bag.** `@doulacloud` on X is already ours. On Bluesky and LinkedIn it is free. On Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook a third party holds an empty, never-posted `doulacloud` account, and Threads inherits the Instagram one. None of the holders has ever posted, so none has a common-law claim, but none of the handles can be simply claimed either.
4. **Filing costs $350 per class** (electronic, Trademark ID Manual identification) and the USPTO currently takes **4.2 months to a first examination** and **9.8 months to registration or abandonment** on average.

The rest of this document is the evidence.

## 1. USPTO: federal trademark search

**Method.** The USPTO's trademark search at [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-information) is a JavaScript application behind AWS WAF; a plain HTTP fetch is refused. The search was run in the real browser on 8 September 2026, and the search service's own JSON responses (`POST https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/prod-stage-v1-0-0/tmsearch`, the same call the page makes) were captured so that the full result sets, not the first page, could be read. The index covers live and dead marks. Wordmark searches were run for `doula` (258 hits, all read), `doulacloud` (0), `doula cloud`, `doulas cloud`, `cloud doula` (each about 8,600 hits, dominated by CLOUD marks; the 300 highest-scoring read), `dula` (18), `doola` (4), and `dhoula` (0). Every hit was then filtered on the wordmark and its pseudo-mark text.

## 2. New York: entities and assumed names

**Department of State, Corporation and Business Entity Database.** The DOS search at [apps.dos.ny.gov/publicInquiry](https://apps.dos.ny.gov/publicInquiry/) states on its own page that it "includes business and not for profit corporations, limited partnerships, limited liability companies, limited liability partnerships, and other miscellaneous businesses" and "also includes assumed name filings for corporations, limited liability companies and limited partnerships." On 8 September 2026 the following searches were run, each with Search Functionality "Contains" and All Statuses, once against the Limited Liability Company list and once against the Corporation list: Entity Name `doula cloud`, Entity Name `doulacloud`, Assumed Name `doula cloud`, Assumed Name `doulacloud`. All eight returned "No business entities were found." A control search for `briandor` returned only BRIAN DORIA LLC and BRIAN DORSEY STUDIOS, LLC, which confirms the search works and that Briandor LLC is not yet on the register.

**Open data mirror.** The same register is published as [Active Corporations: Beginning 1800](https://data.ny.gov/resource/n9v6-gdp6.json) on data.ny.gov (rows last updated 8 September 2026). A query for `current_entity_name` containing both DOULA and CLOUD returned zero rows; 422 active entities contain DOULA, none of them with CLOUD. The query used was `$where=upper(current_entity_name) like '%DOULA%CLOUD%'`.

**The gap the state register cannot close.** A sole proprietor's assumed name (a DBA without an entity behind it) is filed with the county clerk, not with DOS, and no county publishes that index in a searchable form on the state site. Monroe County's clerk index was not searched. This is the one first-party source this document does not reach; a doula trading as "Doula Cloud" as an unincorporated sole proprietor in any New York county would not appear above. The common-law check in section 3 is the mitigation.

**Result: clean.** No New York entity or state-filed assumed name contains "doula cloud" or "doulacloud" on 8 September 2026.

## 3. Common-law use

A common-law claim needs actual use of the name in trade. The check was for any business, app, or site trading as DoulaCloud or Doula Cloud.

**Web.** Searches for `"DoulaCloud"` and `"Doula Cloud"` (with and without app, software, and business) returned no business, product, or site under the name. The doula-software results are the known field: Enginehire, HoneyBook, Dubsado, Doulado, Birthbase, Mobile Doula, Omnify, BloomCare, DoulaConnect. The one exact hit was `doulacloud.app`, which is a parked domain (section 4). Search run 8 September 2026.

**App stores.** Searches limited to apps.apple.com and play.google.com for "Doula Cloud" and "DoulaCloud" returned no app under the name. The nearest names are [Mobile Doula](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mobile-doula/id909265632), [Doula Life](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doula-life/id1382392194), [DoulaConnect](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doulaconnect/id6739744835), and [BloomCare](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doula-client-app-bloomcare/id6768470272). Search run 8 September 2026.

**Wayback Machine.** [doulacloud.com](http://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=doulacloud.com&output=txt) has five captures (May 2024 to July 2025); every one is a redirect or a 403, and the only 200 body, from 26 March 2025, is our own "dou.la URL Shortener" page. [doulacloud.app](http://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=doulacloud.app&output=txt) has two captures (July 2024, July 2025), both the empty GoDaddy parking shell. No capture of either domain ever carried a product.

**Social accounts under the name.** Section 5 lists four third-party accounts named `doulacloud` or DoulaCloud (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook). Every one has zero posts, zero followers, and no bio or contact detail. A reserved handle with no activity is not use in trade.

**Conclusion.** No common-law user of DoulaCloud or Doula Cloud was found. The only prior appearances of the string are empty handle reservations and a parked domain.

## 6. What a federal application costs and how long it takes

**Cost.** From the [USPTO fee schedule](https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/fees-and-payment/uspto-fee-schedule), "Trademark application-related fees", headed "Effective January 19, 2025 (Last revised August 14, 2026)", read 8 September 2026:

| 37 CFR | Fee | Amount | Fee code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2.6(a)(1)(iii) | Base application, per class | **$350.00** | 7017 |
| 2.6(a)(1)(i) | Base application (paper), per class | $850.00 | 6001 |
| 2.6(a)(1)(iv) | Insufficient information (Sections 1 and 44), per class | $100.00 | 7018 |
| 2.6(a)(1)(v) | Using the free-form text box instead of the Trademark ID Manual within Trademark Center to identify goods and services (Sections 1 and 44), per class | $200.00 | 7019 |
| 2.6(a)(1)(vi) | Each additional group of 1,000 characters in the free-form text box beyond the first 1,000 (Sections 1 and 44), per affected class | $200.00 | 7020 |
| 2.6(a)(3)(i)-(ii) | Statement of use (SOU), per class | $150.00 | 7003 |
| 2.6(a)(4)(i)-(ii) | Request for six-month extension for filing an SOU, per class | $125.00 | 7004 |
| 2.6(a)(28)(ii) | Extension of time for filing a response to a pre-registration office action | $125.00 | 7016 |

So the base cost for DOULACLOUD is **$350 per class** when the goods and services are picked from the Trademark ID Manual, and the surcharges apply only when they are not. One class (42, software as a service) is $350; classes 9, 35, 42, and 44 together are $1,400. Because the product has not launched, an application filed before January 2027 would be on an intent-to-use basis, and the registration only issues after a Statement of Use at **$150 per class** more, or a $125-per-class extension if launch slips past the six-month window. The page that used to summarize trademark fees, `uspto.gov/trademarks/fees-payment-information`, is a 404 as of 8 September 2026; the fee schedule above is the USPTO's page of record.

**Timeline.** From the [Trademarks Dashboard](https://www.uspto.gov/dashboard/trademarks/), "Trademarks Data Q3 2026 at a glance", read 8 September 2026:

> First Action Pendency: **4.2 months**. Total Pendency: **9.8 months**.

> "First action pendency measures the average number of months from the date of application filing to the examining attorney's first office action. The target for the current fiscal year is 5 months from filing. The USPTO long term goal for first action pendency is 4.5 months."

> "Total pendency measures the average number of months from date of filing to notice of abandonment, notice of allowance, or registration for applications based on use in that month excluding applications that were previously suspended or were involved in inter partes proceedings at the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board. The target for the current fiscal year is 11 months or less, on average."

> "The target for the current fiscal year, including applications that were previously suspended or were involved in inter partes proceedings at the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board, is 14 months, on average."

Read against the calendar: an application filed in September 2026 would, on the current averages, see its first office action around late January 2027 and reach a notice of allowance around July 2027. Registration itself then waits on the Statement of Use, which cannot be filed until the mark is in use in commerce, that is, after launch. Filing now therefore does not deliver a registration number before January 2027; it delivers a filing date, which is what the handle-reclamation forms in section 5 and the "TM" claim on the marketing site can use in the meantime.
