6. Voice

Founder voice, first person, on every channel through launch. The paragraph and the three sentences are the teaser's own words, as decided on ticket #362. Nothing here is new copy.

If you do this work, you already know where the hours go. Chasing a signature. Remembering who is due when. Working out what you're owed, in a spreadsheet only you understand.

DoulaCloud is one place for all of it, whether it's just you or a dozen doulas. It opens in January 2027. It's not ready yet, and I'd rather tell you that than show you a demo.

Mark · Building DoulaCloud

  • I'm building the thing you keep rebuilding in a spreadsheet.A headline. It names the reader's work, not the product's features.
  • I need an email address I can actually write to.An error. One person speaks, including when something fails.
  • Then nothing from me until January.A promise. It says what will not happen.
  • Say I, never we. One person signs.
  • Use contractions. Write the way the founder talks.
  • Say what is not ready. No claim that the product cannot show.
  • Name work that a solo doula and an agency both do.
  • No pronoun for a Client or a doula. Use the name, or the noun.
