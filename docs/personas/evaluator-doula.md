# Tasha Bell — evaluator doula

- **Archetype**: Prospect deciding whether Doula Cloud is worth switching to
- **Pronouns**: she/her
- **Surface**: the marketing site first, then `/signup`
- **Roles**: none yet — she has no Practice and no membership
- **Entry point**: a search result or a recommendation in a doula Facebook group,
  landing on a marketing site that **does not exist yet**

## Who she is

Tasha runs a two-doula practice in Tucson on a spreadsheet and a shared Google Drive.
Someone in her certification cohort mentioned Doula Cloud. She has fifteen minutes
between Clients and three other tabs open comparing tools.

She has been burned before by software built for medical clinics and bent into a doula
shape. She wants to see the word "doula" and the word "birth plan" before she gives
anyone an email address.

## Why she comes to Doula Cloud

To answer one question fast: is this built for what I actually do, and can I get out
again if it is not?

## Primary journey

From cold arrival to a decision: land on the marketing site, understand what the product
is and what it costs, sign up, create a Practice, and reach a first screen that either
convinces her or does not.

## Done looks like

Either she has a Practice with one test Client in it and intends to come back, or she
has left with a clear reason. Both outcomes close the journey; only the second one is a
failure of the product.

## Watch for

- **Her journey starts on a page that has not been built.** The Hugo marketing site is
  out of scope for this effort, so her first leg is a gap issue, not a test step.
- Pricing is the question she asks second, and there is no live Stripe account to
  answer it.
- Her signup is the same flow as Maya's, but with none of Maya's motivation. Every
  extra field costs her.
- She is the only persona who may legitimately abandon halfway. The journey map must
  record where she stops, not only whether she finishes.
