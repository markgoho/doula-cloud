# Billing is named Credits, Payments is named Getting paid

Before this decision, the Practice-scoped screen where an Owner buys Credits was labeled `Billing`, and the screen where a Practice connects Stripe Connect was labeled `Payments`. Both names described the general subject of money rather than what each screen actually holds, and both hid that the two screens face different counterparties: Credits is Doula Cloud's own billing to the Practice, and Stripe Connect is the Practice getting paid by its own Clients. CONTEXT.md's own **Payment** entry — "money received against an Invoice" — is the tell: the screen labeled Payments lists no Payments and manages a **Connected account** instead, and the Practice's own billing of its Clients already has a home under `Invoices`. Decided on [#256](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/256).

The screens are renamed to the domain terms CONTEXT.md already defines: `Billing` becomes **Credits** (the thing the screen sells, per the **Credit** entry), and `Payments` becomes **Getting paid** (already the Overview hub's own section heading for this screen, and no CONTEXT.md term names Stripe Connect onboarding better). Both labels move together across the nav, the page title, the Settings hub tile, and the Overview hub's link text, so no surface calls a screen a third thing.

The route slugs (`/billing`, `/settings/payments`) stay as they are, on purpose — renaming a URL is a bigger, unrelated commitment (redirects, bookmarks, e2e fixtures keyed on the path) that this ticket's acceptance criteria never asked for. A future reader who notices the label and the URL disagree should read that as this decision, not as an unfinished rename.
