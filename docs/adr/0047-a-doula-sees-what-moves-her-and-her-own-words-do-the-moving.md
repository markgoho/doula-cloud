# A doula sees what moves her, and her own words do the moving

How Doula Cloud may move a person was decided one mechanism at a time, on the product side first. [ADR-0021](0021-govuk-is-the-reference-for-service-patterns.md) keeps "please", "valid", "invalid" and "required" out of every error message, and `app/src/lib/formErrors.usage.spec.ts` fails a commit on them. [ADR-0038](0038-overdue-is-derived-and-notifies-nobody.md) keeps a Client's open Invoice labeled "Not yet paid", because "Overdue" in red on a bill read by a woman whose pregnancy ended in loss is the product shaming her. [#473](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/473) hard-blocks the four undoable actions behind a deliberate, action-named override. [ADR-0017](0017-twelve-columns-a-practice-defined-layer-and-an-engagement-that-is-asked-for.md) keeps pronouns Practice-defined, requires only `given_name`, and carries no honorific column, and the signup screen renders six controls with no consent checkbox and no optional-looking field between them. [ADR-0031](0031-practice-deletion-is-a-thirty-day-window-then-the-erasure-cascade.md) lets an Owner delete the Practice herself, with no call in the way. [ADR-0042](0042-a-practice-pays-per-engagement-and-doula-cloud-is-paid-when-the-doula-is.md) settled one price with every feature and no tier to favor, the price published, the refund of an unspent Credit as the only guarantee, no card until the first purchase, and a rise that never touches a bought Credit. [ADR-0043](0043-a-date-is-a-trigger-only-when-someone-set-it.md) ruled out the countdown and the manufactured deadline, the cadence to the list, and the chase of a paying Practice. [ADR-0044](0044-the-founder-goes-where-doulas-already-gather-and-the-site-is-arrived-at.md) made the site's one ask "start", its proof founding facts only with no testimonial, no count and no trusted-by row, the pilot offer a thing made by hand to a Practice who asks, and the founder a person who speaks about the work with no product-name account before January. [ADR-0045](0045-a-doula-is-heard-before-she-is-chosen.md) claims nothing about a doula until a doula has said it. [ADR-0046](0046-the-product-counts-what-a-practice-did-and-never-who-she-is.md) reads no named person's use and runs no split test. Those records hold the mechanisms. None of them named the test that tells a nudge from a trick, said whether anything time-shaped or supply-shaped ever appears on a page, set the limit the site's stakes and villain carry, said what the site may claim about the product or the founder about a feature, said who writes a testimonial once one is earned, or said whether a recommendation is ever paid for.

On 2026-09-18 the founder answered those questions against the twenty-nine books on the values track, in the sixth grilling session under [ADR-0041](0041-a-book-enters-the-repo-as-evidence-and-becomes-a-rule-only-through-a-decision.md), on [#1376](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1376), from the theme synthesis at [`docs/research/books/themes/persuasion-and-its-limits.md`](../research/books/themes/persuasion-and-its-limits.md). Eight questions over two rounds. Every recommendation was taken as written; where the founder's answer covered part of one, the rest was put back to him and taken. He confirmed the positions record on that ticket. This ADR is the value behind those records: why each position holds, in his reasons, with the evidence weighed and what was rejected. Page citations are as the read-backs in `docs/research/books/` give them, each under its own page rule. A book is evidence here and nowhere the reason.

**A doula sees what moves her, and her own words do the moving. A technique is open to Doula Cloud only if the founder would say, on the same surface, what it is and why it is there, and a doula who read that would still be glad she acted; what the site says about her is what she said, and nothing about the product is claimed in her voice that she did not say.**

## The decision

### One test on every surface, and the founder names himself as the builder

A technique is open to Doula Cloud only if the founder would state, on that same surface, what it is and why it is there, and a doula who read the statement would still be glad she acted. Anything that works only while unseen is a trick. The surfaces are the site, the app, every email in either voice, and any room he speaks in, his own Facebook profile and a community thread included; on every one of them he names himself as the builder on the first mention. Thomas's disclosure test is the half that can be checked before a page ships, and Godin's two-sided test is the half that says what the disclosure is for; held together they are one rule. Its first application was decided as form: the January site carries no pop-up, no exit-intent overlay, no sticky bar and no chat widget, because GOV.UK, the reference for any screen that asks a person for something (ADR-0021), has none and the site's one ask is "start" (ADR-0044 P6); and the primary button is the design system's, one primary action per screen, its color a token in `app/src/lib/tokens.css` ([#409](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/409)), so nobody tunes it as a lever. The founder's reason: "yes to all recommendations"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that the product's record is built on a Practice knowing what is done with her data and her Clients ([ADR-0016](0016-teaser-analytics-are-cookieless-and-the-channel-rides-on-the-form.md), [ADR-0022](0022-one-activity-log-with-a-subject-and-three-kinds-of-actor.md), [ADR-0030](0030-no-third-party-open-or-click-tracking-in-notification-email.md)), so a site that hides its persuasion from the same woman contradicts the product behind it, and that a recommendation from a person who turns out to be the seller is the sneaking around Godin names.

Weighed for: _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s disclosure test, whether the designer would disclose why the layout steers ([design-for-cognitive-bias](../research/books/design-for-cognitive-bias.md), ch. 4, pp. 87–88); _This Is Marketing_'s two-sided test, marketer and consumer both knowing what is happening and both satisfied, with "doing my job" refused as a defense ([this-is-marketing](../research/books/this-is-marketing.md), Ch. 23, PDF pp. 196–197), and its marketer who sneaks around and steals attention and a faux trust that does not last (Ch. 18, PDF pp. 159–160); _Lean Marketing_'s rule that no tactic is used to deceive ([lean-marketing](../research/books/lean-marketing.md), Chapter 12, p. 204) and its marketing the market would pay to receive (Chapter 1, p. 21); _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s separation of eliciting from convincing ([the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany](../research/books/the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany.md), Chapter 3, pp. 187–189); _The Mom Test_'s reason for keeping the idea out of an interview ([the-mom-test](../research/books/the-mom-test.md), ch. 1, p. 13), turned around as the reason to name it when selling; _Marketing Made Simple_'s own concession that the pop-up is annoying and its no-X close feels like clickbait ([marketing-made-simple](../research/books/marketing-made-simple.md), ch. 6, p. 123). Rejected: _Zero to One_'s sales that work best hidden and the person who thinks himself immune as doubly deceived ([zero-to-one](../research/books/zero-to-one.md), ch. 11, PDF pp. 97–98); _Software as a Science_'s lead magnet that positions the platform as the only viable solution and its credit card asked for while the prospect pulls over ([software-as-a-science](../research/books/software-as-a-science.md), ch. 4, PDF p. 93; ch. 5, PDF p. 114); _Landing Page Hot Tips_ as a set, because it states no test; _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s Buy Now button in a brighter color than every other button, repeated as the page scrolls ([building-a-storybrand-2](../research/books/building-a-storybrand-2.md), ch. 8, PDF pp. 116–117; ch. 14, PDF pp. 178–180), answered by the pattern rather than tuned; effect alone as the test.

### Nothing time-shaped or supply-shaped on any page or in any send

Nothing time-shaped or supply-shaped appears on any page or in any send. A real end is a fact and may be stated where it is already true and relevant: the founding grant's close is in the pilot terms, "the one thing the pilot offers that January does not", and in the founder's own offer to a Practice who asks (ADR-0044 P1); it gets no countdown, no color, no "only", and no second mention, and it never goes on the January site, which opens after the grant has closed. No launch discount, because there is one price (ADR-0042 P3, P4) and a discount to the list is a time-limited price by another door. No cap on pilot Practices, because capacity is not limited and a cap that is not real fails the test above in one sentence. The countdown and the deadline that is not real were settled on [#1372](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1372) as ADR-0043 P6 and are held here. The founder's reason: "go with your recommendation"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him.

Weighed for: _This Is Marketing_'s canyon of being left out that did not exist before the marketer created it, and the going-out-of-business sale whose logic the author calls elusive (Ch. 10, PDF pp. 100–101); _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s framing effect, the frame superseding the facts (ch. 2, pp. 34–35), and its disclosure test (ch. 4, pp. 87–88); _The Voltage Effect_'s refusal of the manufactured loss that trades on reputation ([the-voltage-effect](../research/books/the-voltage-effect.md), Chapter 6, PDF pp. 134–136); _The Mom Test_'s commitment that is not strong-arming (ch. 5, pp. 69, 71); ADR-0043 P6's reason, that "when it opens" is an event and needs no deadline invented beside it; ADR-0042 P7's rule that no price is ever promised for life and none is time-limited. Rejected: _Landing Page Hot Tips_'s haste by time-limited discount, demand by limited supply, and soft launch by discount to a select audience ([landing-page-hot-tips](../research/books/landing-page-hot-tips.md), Tips #24, #51, #76; PDF pp. 56, 98–99, 142); _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s scarcity in the sales email's call to action and its onetime offer (ch. 15, PDF pp. 207–208); _Marketing Made Simple_'s conditional limited-time offer (ch. 9, p. 149); _The Voltage Effect_'s clawback, the money given before it is earned so it is the worker's to lose (Chapter 6, PDF pp. 143–144, 147–148), as a technique for customers, because its obligations are written for employees and none transfers to a stranger on a page.

### The stakes are her words, no surface shames her, and a quote needs her yes

The site's stakes are a doula's own words, past tense, about what she lost the last time: the intake she retyped, the night she rewrote notes, the message she could not find. The sentence comes from a call recorded in `docs/research/pilot-agency-interviews.md`. The villain is a thing she named, the spreadsheet, the group text, the notes in three places, and never a person, never her competence, and never a family harmed. If no doula names a loss, the site has no stakes section. No surface, the site included, is built to make a doula feel behind, late or less: ADR-0038's refusal of shame extends from the Client's invoice to every page a Practice reads, and a fact she can act on is shown as a fact. A stakes quote names the problem and speaks for her, about the time before the product; a testimonial names the product and speaks for it; so ADR-0044 P6, proof is founding facts only and no testimonial on the January site, stands as written. Consent is asked at the end of every call on [#994](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/994)'s guide, as two separate questions with leave to say no to either: may her words go in the notes file, and may a sentence of hers go on the site. A quote on the site is unattributed by default and attributed only if she asks. The notes file carries no name and no Practice name unless she said yes to that as well, so a public repository holds nothing she did not agree to. The founder's reason: "go with recs" and "sure"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that the product will never show a doula a picture of a birth going wrong to sell her software for it, and that a doula who said what the spreadsheet cost her is telling the truth, so leaving her sentence off the page to be safe is the shouting sign's opposite error.

Weighed for: _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s truth condition on drama and its villain that must be real, with the reader told never to be a fearmonger (ch. 5, PDF pp. 72, 75–76); _Marketing Made Simple_'s ASPCA commercials the author himself turned off, with the guess that a lighter version would have done better (ch. 5, p. 74); _This Is Marketing_'s fear as a dream killer and shame as the status killer, held as the description of what is refused (Ch. 10, PDF p. 99; Ch. 11, PDF p. 108), and its permission that neither a held address nor fine print gives (Ch. 17, PDF pp. 152–153); _The Voltage Effect_'s refusal of public shaming inside a business, a recipe for a toxic culture (Chapter 6, PDF pp. 134–136); _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s reactance, the polite sign that draws less graffiti than the shouting one (ch. 1, pp. 4–5), and its pause before every request for personal information (ch. 4, pp. 74–75); _Competing Against Luck_'s examination-room paper designed for function that manufactures anxiety ([competing-against-luck](../research/books/competing-against-luck.md), ch. 2, PDF p. 38; ch. 4, PDF pp. 83–84); _Forget the Funnel_'s words a customer arrives with, collected only by open-ended questions ([forget-the-funnel](../research/books/forget-the-funnel.md), ch. 3, PDF pp. 41–42); _Copyhackers: Uplift_'s copy written from the customer's own words ([copyhackers-uplift](../research/books/copyhackers-uplift.md), Section 2, p. 20); ADR-0045 P5, nothing about a doula claimed until a doula said it. Rejected: _This Is Marketing_'s manufactured tension as the craft (Ch. 10, PDF pp. 100–101); _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s "almost never" too many stakes and its fearmongering as the problem 99.9 percent of leaders do not have (ch. 9, PDF pp. 131–134), because a book's estimate of who overuses fear is not a limit; _Software as a Science_'s stretching the gap, pushing on a pain until it is unignorable (ch. 5, PDF p. 106); no stakes at all; founder-written stakes from the calls, because he did not hear them as he would write them; a stakes quote read as a testimonial; a quoted sentence held until after launch, because it is not proof of the product and waiting buys nothing; the notes file kept out of the public repository, because ADR-0045 put it there and consent asked at the call is the smaller change.

### The site states facts in verbs, and no feature is promised to win a Practice

The site says what the product does today, on trunk, what it costs, who built it and where, the guarantee in the pilot terms' own words, and that Client records are held under a HIPAA business associate agreement, because each is a sentence a doula can check. No adjective about itself, "easy", "simple", "secure", "the best", appears outside a quoted doula's words; no award, no statistic, no trusted-by row (ADR-0044 P6). No feature is promised to win a Practice: what she asks for goes in the notes file beside the call as evidence (ADR-0045), and what the founder intends is said as intent with no date, which is ADR-0043's rule that a date is a trigger only when someone set it, turned toward a promise. A Practice who asks "will it do X" hears "not today", then what the product does instead, then whether he intends it and never when. The founder's reason: "go with your rec"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that "easy" from the seller is an opinion of its own value and "easy" from her is proof, and that the agency's pilot is on the grant and asks nothing ([#444](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/444)), so there is no order Blank's exception would be buying.

Weighed for: _Obviously Awesome_'s rule that an opinion of one's own value does not count as proof and third-party validation is what counts ([obviously-awesome](../research/books/obviously-awesome.md), Components, pp. 47–48; Step 5, pp. 81–82); _Copyhackers: Uplift_'s copy from the customer's own words outperforming copy about the product (Section 2, p. 20; Section 4, p. 35); _Zero to One_'s surface techniques that do not work without underlying substance (ch. 5, PDF pp. 40–41); _Start Small, Stay Small_'s product that still has to be good ([start-small-stay-small](../research/books/start-small-stay-small.md), Ch. 1, p. 5; Ch. 2, p. 40); _This Is Marketing_'s overpromise that lowers the cost of the first click and backfires (Ch. 19, PDF p. 164); _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s trap of promising unique extensions and its incomplete release that must not be etched in customers' minds as the finished product (Chapter 4, pp. 292, 294, 309); _Marketing Made Simple_'s authority unrelated to the customer's problem left off, and its reader with no testimonials and no awards told not to worry (ch. 5, pp. 84–85, 89); _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s technology that helps a small business look larger and does not replace great products and service ([the-1-page-marketing-plan](../research/books/the-1-page-marketing-plan.md), Chapter 6, p. 186). Rejected: _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s guide that may talk about itself all day long provided it tells only the supporting parts (ch. 6, PDF p. 98), and its statistics and awards as ways to show competency (ch. 6, PDF p. 99); _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s allowance that such promises are sometimes essential to get an order or two (Chapter 4, p. 309), because the order it buys is one the grant already covers; value adjectives where the founder believes them; a dated feature promise where he would build it anyway.

### A testimonial is her words, asked once, after a spent Credit

A testimonial is her words only: written or spoken by her, quoted verbatim, cut only for length and with her approval of the cut, with her written consent to her name and her Practice's name. It is asked for once, in conversation, after she has spent a Credit on a real Client, which is the value she bought and the ledger's own act (ADR-0046 P2); never from inside the app, never on a cue from product data (ADR-0046 P5), never drafted for her approval. A no or a silence is the answer and there is no second ask. She may withdraw it at any time and it comes down. Consent is an email she wrote, kept in the Doula Cloud Google account beside the record of the call; a withdrawal is honored on receipt with no reason asked, in the pilot terms' own shape for a refund. No Credits change hands for a review, because the grant already gave her the Credits and asked nothing (#444). When a testimonial is earned is ADR-0044 P6's, after launch, held here. The founder's reason: "go with your rec"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that a sentence the founder wrote and she signed is his opinion of his own value in her mouth.

Weighed for: _Lean Marketing_'s first advice to a business with no social proof, not to be dishonest and invent it, and its honest route of the product given free to a select group for an honest review (Chapter 14, pp. 237–238); _Obviously Awesome_'s third-party validation as what counts (Step 5, pp. 81–82); _Copyhackers: Uplift_'s customer's words (Section 2, p. 20); _The Mom Test_'s commitment that is not strong-arming and the founder with no claim on anyone's contacts (ch. 5, pp. 69, 71); _This Is Marketing_'s permission as anticipated, personal and relevant (Ch. 17, PDF pp. 152–153); _Company of One_'s cash incentive that reduces trust because profit is then seen as the whole reason ([company-of-one](../research/books/company-of-one.md), ch. 10, PDF p. 119); ADR-0044 P6; ADR-0046 P5. Rejected: _Marketing Made Simple_'s testimonial drafted for the customer and sent back for approval, which the authors say is not making something up (ch. 5, pp. 86–87); _Software as a Science_'s testimonial pre-written from the customer's own data, coached through situation, struggle and solution, and directed to whichever review site needs authority (ch. 8, PDF pp. 174–181); _Building a StoryBrand 2.0_'s about three short testimonials as a target (ch. 6, PDF p. 99), because a count of testimonials is a number on nobody's page (ADR-0046 P3); _Lean Marketing_'s exchange made formal, because the pilot already is the free product to a select group and its terms ask nothing back.

### No money, no Credit and no title for a recommendation

No money and no Credit changes hands for a recommendation, in either direction. No title or public status is conferred on a Practice for recommending. The founder asks a Practice for a name at most once, in conversation, after she has bought again, and a no is the answer. A doula who recommends the product does it for her own reasons, and the moment a Credit rides on it her colleague cannot tell which reason it was. "Founding Practice" on a public page is the anointment even when she agreed to it, and it makes her leaving a public event. Bought-again is the one act in the ledger that says she chose the product a second time with her own money (ADR-0046 P2); a name asked for before that is a favor asked of a stranger on a grant. The founder's reason, in his words: "no money, no credit", and "yes both parts".

Weighed for: _Company of One_'s cash incentive that reduces trust, and its rule that no incentive creates advocates without a good product and good service first (ch. 10, PDF p. 119); _Lean Marketing_'s case against cash for informal referrals, and its note that in a formal referral relationship disclosure is often legally required and incentivized referrals are sometimes illegal (Chapter 14, p. 243); _The Mom Test_'s founder with no claim on anyone's contacts (ch. 5, p. 71); _This Is Marketing_'s permission (Ch. 17, PDF pp. 152–153); ADR-0042 P4, one price and nothing sold beside it, so a Credit for a referral is a price cut by another door; ADR-0046 P5, no ask cued by data; ADR-0031, leaving is self-serve with no call in the way. Rejected: _Company of One_'s discounts, swag and double-sided offers as the incentives that do not reduce trust (ch. 10, PDF p. 119), as they would apply here, because a Credit is the price and the price is one; _Software as a Science_'s referral asked right after the customer says something nice, on commitment and consistency, and its anointed advocate whose status is announced publicly so she is least likely to churn (ch. 8, PDF pp. 174–181), because a status announced to keep her from leaving is a lever on the same woman ADR-0031 lets leave; the ask left to judgment in the moment with no rule about once or about bought-again.

### What the record had already settled, held as recorded

The favored plan and the contrast effect: one price, every feature, no tiers (ADR-0042 P4; [#439](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/439)), so _Landing Page Hot Tips_'s tiers with one suggested (Tips #14, #50; PDF pp. 39–41, 95–97), _Marketing Made Simple_'s three prices with the middle one chosen (ch. 5, p. 103), _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s single pricing option as an error (Chapter 6, p. 191; Chapter 8, p. 244), _Start Small, Stay Small_'s three tiers and every price ending in 7, 8 or 9 (Ch. 3, p. 99), and _Software as a Science_'s features fenced across plan levels are rejected on it, with _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s contrast effect (ch. 4, pp. 87–88) and _Company of One_'s fixed, published prices as the trust move (ch. 10, PDF p. 117) in agreement. Friction: the four undoable actions are hard-blocked with an action-named override (#473), leaving is self-serve (ADR-0031), no card is taken until the first purchase (ADR-0042 P2), and the pilot offer is made only to a Practice who asks (ADR-0044 P1), so _Software as a Science_'s credit card on the call and its cancellation that requires a phone call (ch. 5, PDF p. 114; ch. 7, PDF p. 163) are rejected, with _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s friction that is sometimes good (ch. 2, pp. 35–36), _Competing Against Luck_'s obligation to convey who should not hire a product (ch. 6, PDF p. 131), and _The Mom Test_'s ban on strong-arming (ch. 5, p. 69) in agreement; whether a one-line "who this is not for" sentence is written is [#868](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/868)'s copy call (ADR-0044 P6). Urgency: the countdown and the manufactured deadline (ADR-0043 P6), the cadence to the list and the chase of a paying Practice (ADR-0043 P2, P4), against _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s marketing to the database until people buy or die (Chapter 5, pp. 155–156) and _The SaaS Playbook_'s founder email within ten minutes of a cancellation ([the-saas-playbook](../research/books/the-saas-playbook.md), 80/20 SaaS Metrics, p. 161). The lead magnet, the email address as the site's first goal, the free report and the drip (ADR-0044 P6). Selling what does not exist: the teaser's fixed copy says "coming January 2027" ([#362](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/362)), which is _Start Small, Stay Small_'s "Coming soon" emblem (Ch. 2, p. 85); no money is taken before the first purchase (ADR-0042 P2), which answers _Entrepreneur Revolution_'s money set aside so it can be returned ([entrepreneur-revolution](../research/books/entrepreneur-revolution.md), Chapter 4, PDF p. 100); the pilot is private and its terms are unlisted ([#421](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/421), #444), which is _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s invite-only site with no press releases or public demos ([the-startup-owners-manual](../research/books/the-startup-owners-manual.md), Web/Mobile, Ch. 5, PDF p. 420), and its investors who encourage a launch of a product that does not exist (Web/Mobile, Ch. 4, PDF p. 394) have no case here. Grandfathering and the announced rise (ADR-0042 P7), the guarantee (ADR-0042 P6), and price as a signal, with _This Is Marketing_'s lowered price that does the opposite of building trust (Ch. 16, PDF p. 149), against ADR-0042 P3. The retargeting pixel that tags visitors into an advertising audience (_Software as a Science_, ch. 4, PDF p. 92) is out on ADR-0016, which stores nothing on a visitor's device, and ADR-0044 P4, which buys no attention. Reactance in copy: "please", "valid", "invalid" and "required" never appear in an error (ADR-0021). Notational bias: pronouns Practice-defined, only `given_name` required, no honorific column, and no optional-looking field on the signup screen (ADR-0017; `app/src/routes/(signed-out)/signup/+page.svelte`), which is _Design for Cognitive Bias_'s chapter on what a form lets a person express, already applied (ch. 4, pp. 72–76, 87). The interview rules of _The Mom Test_, _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_ and _Thinking in Bets_ are ADR-0045 P3 and P5. Split tests are ADR-0046 P6, and the Win/Ask timed by the company's own usage data is ADR-0046 P5. The category declaration is positioning, on [#999](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/999). Build in public with screens was honored in the founder's own voice on ADR-0044 P3. No position is taken on _The Voltage Effect_'s strategic apology (Chapter 9, PDF p. 212), _The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_'s authenticity ([the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant](../research/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant.md), Building Wealth, pp. 41, 44), or _Buy Back Your Time_'s rule that the receiver decides ([buy-back-your-time](../research/books/buy-back-your-time.md), ch. 12, PDF pp. 210, 212), because no conflict in the synthesis asks for one. On the evidence, as ADR-0045 and ADR-0046 recorded: no book's uncited figure is the reason for any position above, not _Marketing Made Simple_'s middle-item finding attributed to what certified guides have discovered (ch. 5, p. 103), not _Software as a Science_'s audited sales calls, not _Start Small, Stay Small_'s rule it calls stupid and keeps because it works (Ch. 3, p. 99); every claim in the books is its author's own practice, weighed as experience, and _The Voltage Effect_'s field experiments are weighed as the one set built on replication.

## What was considered instead

**Thiel's hidden sale, or effect alone as the test.** Rejected because the product's whole record rests on a Practice knowing what is done with her data, and a site that hides its persuasion from the same woman contradicts the product behind it.

**The grant's close named on the January site or in the opening broadcast as a reason to start.** Rejected because the site opens after the grant has closed and has nothing to hurry anyone toward.

**A launch discount to the waitlist, or a stated cap on pilot Practices.** Rejected because there is one price and a discount is a time-limited price by another door, and a cap that is not real fails the test in one sentence.

**Founder-written stakes inside Miller's salt limit, or Godin's crafted tension, or no stakes at all.** Rejected because the founder did not hear the loss as he would write it, because tension a marketer creates is the canyon that did not exist before, and because a doula who said what the spreadsheet cost her is telling the truth.

**A stakes quote read as a testimonial, kept off the site until after launch.** Rejected because a sentence about the problem proves nothing about the product, and nobody claims it does.

**The notes file kept out of the public repository.** Rejected because ADR-0045 put it there, and consent asked at the call is the smaller change.

**Miller's authority section written by the founder, value adjectives he believes, or a dated feature promise where he would build it anyway.** Rejected because "easy" from the seller is an opinion of its own value, and the one order Blank's exception would buy is already covered by the grant.

**A testimonial drafted for her approval, pre-written from her usage data, or bought with Credits for an honest review.** Rejected because a sentence he wrote and she signed is his opinion in her mouth, no named person's use is read, and the pilot's Credits already came with nothing asked back.

**Credits both ways for a referral, cash, or an anointed advocate.** Rejected because a Credit is the price and the price is one, cash removes the reason her colleague could trust, and a status announced to keep her from leaving is a lever on the woman ADR-0031 lets leave.

**The ask for a name left to judgment in the moment.** Put to the founder and taken as recommended instead: once, in conversation, after she has bought again.
