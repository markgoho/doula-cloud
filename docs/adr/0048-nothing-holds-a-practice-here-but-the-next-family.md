# Nothing holds a Practice here but the next family

What keeps a Practice on Doula Cloud was decided one mechanism at a time before anyone named it. [ADR-0042](0042-a-practice-pays-per-engagement-and-doula-cloud-is-paid-when-the-doula-is.md) made the Engagement the unit, one Credit each, with no subscription, no minimum and no recurring element, one price with every feature, the price published, an unspent Credit refunded for three years with no reason asked, and a rise that never touches a bought Credit. [ADR-0043](0043-a-date-is-a-trigger-only-when-someone-set-it.md) ruled that a paying Practice is never chased and that a Practice that starts nothing hears once, fourteen days after her signup. [#288](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/288) built the archive an Owner downloads, one ZIP of CSVs any spreadsheet reads. [ADR-0031](0031-practice-deletion-is-a-thirty-day-window-then-the-erasure-cascade.md) lets an Owner delete the Practice herself, through a thirty-day window with no call in the way. [ADR-0046](0046-the-product-counts-what-a-practice-did-and-never-who-she-is.md) reads no named person's use and counts only Credits bought and Credits spent. [ADR-0047](0047-a-doula-sees-what-moves-her-and-her-own-words-do-the-moving.md) forbids a feature promised to win a Practice and any money, Credit or title for a recommendation. [#172](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/172) ruled every v1 feature in or out against three tests, the replacement test, the first-Engagement test "using only Doula Cloud", and the paid test; [#173](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/173) sorted nine competing tools into table stakes and gaps; [#995](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/995) found ten of eleven claim "one place", every one sells a monthly per-seat subscription and none prices per Engagement. [ADR-0008](0008-employment-type-gates-the-practice-attachment-gates-the-engagement.md) gates the Practice by employment type and the Engagement by Attachment, and `CONTEXT.md` makes one person one Staff record across her Practices, with an Offer that may carry an Invitation and nothing browsable. [ADR-0018](0018-templates-are-a-design-system-layer-with-two-named-exits.md) made the empty state of the overview hub a required prop, because the empty Practice was the evaluator's abandon point. Those records hold the mechanisms. None of them named what keeps a Practice here once she has tried it, said whether leaving with everything is a commitment or the current state, said whether "one place" is ever a build goal, gave the rule that adds a feature after launch, said whether staying rests on the founder, said what the first screen asks a new Practice to do, or said what the founder holds when a competitor prices per Engagement.

On 2026-09-18 the founder answered those questions against the twenty-nine books on the values track, in the seventh grilling session under [ADR-0041](0041-a-book-enters-the-repo-as-evidence-and-becomes-a-rule-only-through-a-decision.md), on [#1377](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1377), from the theme synthesis at [`docs/research/books/themes/the-moat.md`](../research/books/themes/the-moat.md). Nine questions over two rounds. Five recommendations were taken as written; one was corrected by a fact the founder holds about how the work is done; one he reopened in his own words, and the answer put back to him in round two was taken. He confirmed the positions record on that ticket. This ADR is the value behind those records: why each position holds, in his reasons, with the evidence weighed and what was rejected. Page citations are as the read-backs in `docs/research/books/` give them, each under its own page rule. A book is evidence here and nowhere the reason.

**Nothing holds a Practice here but the next family. To hold her is to make leaving cost more than staying, and the product refuses it; to keep her is to be worth a Credit the next time a family comes, and that is the whole of the product's work. What a competitor cannot copy without unmaking itself is the model, Doula Cloud paid when the doula is, and the refusals that follow from it; the feature list follows from the Engagement and from nothing else.**

## The decision

### Nothing holds her, "keep" is the word, and the moat is the model

Nothing holds a Practice here but the next family. "Hold" is refused as the product's word and "keep" is the word: to hold her is to make leaving cost more than staying, to keep her is to be worth a Credit the next time a family comes. The product has no moat in the books' sense and builds none. Each of _The SaaS Playbook_'s four is already refused by a record: integrations by [#174](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/174), which ruled third-party calendar sync out of v1, and [ADR-0007](0007-connect-account-state-is-two-capabilities-and-a-requirements-list.md), which makes Stripe a rail; brand by [ADR-0044](0044-the-founder-goes-where-doulas-already-gather-and-the-site-is-arrived-at.md) P3, no product-name account before January; owned traffic by ADR-0043 P2 and ADR-0044 P5, the waitlist hears once and the site is arrived at; switching costs by #288, ADR-0031 and ADR-0042 P1. A feature built to hold her fails ADR-0047 P1's test in one sentence, "this is here so you cannot leave". What a competitor cannot copy without unmaking itself is the model, Doula Cloud paid when the doula is (ADR-0042 P1), since a subscription business that prices per Engagement unprices its own base, and the refusals that follow from it: never chased (ADR-0043 P4), never read (ADR-0046 P5), free to leave (the third position below). All of it is written in a public repository. The founder is not the moat, and the Engagement, the Care Plan and the on-call view are what follows from the model, not what holds her. The founder's reason, in his words: "I guess nothing holds her, but shouldn't we be thinking about what we could do to hold her?", which the session answered by splitting the two words and putting the second position to him, and "love it!" when it was.

Weighed for: _Competing Against Luck_'s competitive advantage conferred by processes rather than by resources or products, because products are copyable and processes are not, and its IKEA, a business with no secrets that nobody copies ([competing-against-luck](../research/books/competing-against-luck.md), ch. 7, PDF pp. 140–142; ch. 6, PDF pp. 121–122); _This Is Marketing_'s trust, located in what was done about a refund, about lost data and about a plant closing rather than in what was said ([this-is-marketing](../research/books/this-is-marketing.md), Ch. 18, PDF pp. 159–160); _Obviously Awesome_'s unique attribute that can be a delivery model or a business model as well as a feature ([obviously-awesome](../research/books/obviously-awesome.md), Components, pp. 46–47; Step 5, pp. 80–82); _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s innovation that need not be in the product and can sit in pricing ([the-1-page-marketing-plan](../research/books/the-1-page-marketing-plan.md), Chapter 7, p. 211); _Lean Marketing_'s product as "just a delivery mechanism" ([lean-marketing](../research/books/lean-marketing.md), Chapter 3, p. 62); _The Voltage Effect_'s ceiling on a business resting on an uncopyable person, Jamie Oliver scaling because the secret was the ingredients rather than the chef ([the-voltage-effect](../research/books/the-voltage-effect.md), Chapter 3, PDF pp. 69–72); _Jobs To Be Done_'s comparison of competitors' feature sets as a waste of time ([jobs-to-be-done](../research/books/jobs-to-be-done.md), ch. 4 §VI, pp. 103, 105); ADR-0042 P1's own reason, "when doula does a birth, doulacloud gets paid". Rejected: _The SaaS Playbook_'s four moats as a set ([the-saas-playbook](../research/books/the-saas-playbook.md), Market, pp. 57–61), because each is refused by a record; _Jobs To Be Done_'s long-term strategy of the entire job on one platform (ch. 2, p. 58; ch. 4 §III, p. 92; §X, p. 122), because ten of eleven competitors already claim it (#995) and its own threshold, that customers switch only for a product upwards of 20 percent better (ch. 3, p. 78), makes the claim table stakes; _Company of One_'s personality as the number-one factor in a survey sent to more than ten thousand of the author's own customers ([company-of-one](../research/books/company-of-one.md), ch. 6, PDF p. 77) and _The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_'s authenticity nobody can compete with ([the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant](../research/books/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant.md), Building Wealth, pp. 41, 44; Saving Yourself, pp. 158–159) as the moat, on List's ceiling, while the founder's own voice stays as ADR-0044 P3 has it; _Zero to One_'s proprietary technology at least ten times better than its closest substitute ([zero-to-one](../research/books/zero-to-one.md), ch. 5, PDF p. 38), because no rota is ten times anything and ADR-0047 P4 forbids the claim; _Entrepreneur Revolution_'s core offer de-commoditized so that it cannot be price-compared ([entrepreneur-revolution](../research/books/entrepreneur-revolution.md), Chapter 5, PDF pp. 118–120), because the price is published on purpose (ADR-0042 P5) and one number invites the comparison; _Make_'s start in a micro niche too small for anyone else ([make-the-bootstrappers-handbook](../research/books/make-the-bootstrappers-handbook.md), Idea, pp. 25–28), held only as Rochester being the first room (ADR-0044 P2) and never as a reason she stays. Held as the position's risk: _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s customer won on price who is lost on price (Chapter 8, pp. 240–241), answered by the fact that a Credit is not a discount, since a busy agency pays per birth what a subscription charges per month and more, and the shape is the difference, not the sum.

### Five things keep her, and the fifth is a person who answers

The product keeps her by doing five things, four already in the record and one new. Her first Engagement runs (the sixth position below). Every family after costs one Credit and nothing between, and a rise never touches a bought one (ADR-0042 P1, P7). The product does what her Engagement needs and nothing else (the fourth position below). She is never surprised: never chased (ADR-0043 P4), never read (ADR-0046 P5), never shamed ([ADR-0038](0038-overdue-is-derived-and-notifies-nobody.md)), and free to leave with everything (the third position below). And a person answers: the founder himself answers `hello@doula.cloud`, with no ticket system and no bot in front of him, through the pilot and the first year. How many hours that is, and what happens when he is asleep or away, is [#1378](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1378)'s to set; this ADR records that a person is the promise, which the pilot terms already publish as "reaches a person" (ADR-0044 P6). The founder's reason, in his words: "love it!"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that the person answering is the one thing on the list a competitor with a help desk cannot copy by adding a feature.

Weighed for: _Company of One_'s structural advantage of a company of one, that its service does not scale and does not have to, CDBaby answering every call within two rings and Basecamp answering support within fifteen minutes, and its renewals as the metric worth watching (ch. 7, PDF pp. 86–87; ch. 4, PDF pp. 55–56); _Lean Marketing_'s customers who leave less for something done wrong than for having no "reason to stay" (Chapter 14, pp. 230–231), answered with reasons that are the job done and not reasons manufactured beside it; _Competing Against Luck_'s three layers, uncovering the job, creating the desired experiences and integrating around the job, and its purpose brand built by doing the job rather than by advertising (ch. 6, PDF pp. 121–122, 132–134); _Forget the Funnel_'s success gap, the disconnect between what ideal customers need and what the current experience provides ([forget-the-funnel](../research/books/forget-the-funnel.md), ch. 8, PDF pp. 110–111), closed by the sixth position; _Software as a Science_'s arithmetic that halving churn beats raising sales by half ([software-as-a-science](../research/books/software-as-a-science.md), ch. 6, PDF pp. 127–130), held as the arithmetic, the founder's hours being his own decision above; _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s retention held far cheaper than acquisition and planned before acquisition succeeds ([the-startup-owners-manual](../research/books/the-startup-owners-manual.md), Web/Mobile, Ch. 3, PDF p. 341); _The Voltage Effect_'s compliance, meaning use, as a non-negotiable (Chapter 3, PDF pp. 75–77), which [#243](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/243) already watches for in the Practice that keeps a birth off the system; _Start Small, Stay Small_'s competition that shortens the period a product can be left on autopilot ([start-small-stay-small](../research/books/start-small-stay-small.md), Ch. 7, p. 192). Rejected: _Lean Marketing_'s engineered reasons to stay and _Company of One_'s out-sharing and teaching as a keep lever (ch. 9, PDF pp. 106–113), because a list the product may not nurture (ADR-0043 P2) and a site with one ask (ADR-0044 P6) leave them no surface; _This Is Marketing_'s permission test, that people notice and ask where the sender went once the sending stops (Ch. 17, PDF pp. 152–154), because the product sends nothing on a schedule for anyone to miss; a response time stated here, because the hours are #1378's; nothing beyond the model, because the person answering is the one thing a competitor cannot copy by adding a feature.

### She leaves with everything, nothing is built to make leaving hurt, and the deletion screen asks one question

A Practice can leave at any time with everything she put in, in a form she can read without Doula Cloud, and nothing is ever built whose reason is to make leaving cost more. The commitment is tested by ADR-0047 P1's sentence: "this is here so leaving hurts" cannot be said on the screen, and a doula who read it would not be glad. The archive is one ZIP of CSVs (#288), deletion is hers with no call in the way (ADR-0031), there is no subscription to cancel (ADR-0042 P1) and an unspent Credit refunds for three years (ADR-0042 P6). One order the window imposes is a fact the restore screen states: the export route is not one of the window's two exemptions, so the archive is taken before deletion starts or after a restore. The screen where she starts deletion asks one question, "What are you moving to?", free text, marked optional, asked once with no second ask, kept with the deletion record beside who started it and when, and read by the founder by hand the way ADR-0046 P3's page is. It is an act of hers and not a send of ours, so ADR-0043 P4 is untouched, and it is in the present tense because she is doing it, which is [ADR-0045](0045-a-doula-is-heard-before-she-is-chosen.md) P3's rule. No email follows a deletion. The founder's reason, in his words: "should be able to export her data if she wants" and "agreed".

Weighed for: _The Voltage Effect_'s finding that a membership scales only where it gives access to goods not readily available without it, and that riders who dual-app switch at almost no cost (Chapter 2, PDF pp. 48–49, 52–53), read as a switching cost that is not real holding nobody; _This Is Marketing_'s trust located in what was done about lost data (Ch. 18, PDF p. 160); _Competing Against Luck_'s hiring anything new as firing something, with loss aversion weighing about twice as heavily as gain (ch. 5, PDF pp. 93–95), the cost she paid once to arrive and will weigh again before she leaves; _Company of One_'s rule that no incentive creates advocates without a good product and good service first (ch. 10, PDF pp. 118–119); _Start Marketing the Day You Start Coding_'s instruction to ask customers what they would use instead ([start-marketing-the-day-you-start-coding](../research/books/start-marketing-the-day-you-start-coding.md), "Four Things I Learned", pp. 150–152); _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s customer with a genuine complaint as an intelligence asset (Chapter 8, p. 260); _Company of One_'s figure that only 4 percent of dissatisfied customers complain (ch. 7, PDF pp. 85–86); the evaluator persona's own question, whether she can get out again (`docs/personas/evaluator-doula.md`). Rejected: _The SaaS Playbook_'s high switching costs as a moat (Market, pp. 57–61), because a moat dug behind a woman who trusted the product with her Clients' records is the sneaking around ADR-0047 P1 refuses; _Software as a Science_'s Baremetrics, which removed self-service cancellation, required a call, kept 15 percent of the leavers and brought churn from 13 percent back to 5 (ch. 7, PDF pp. 163–164), because ADR-0031 settled that no call stands in her way and fifteen percent kept by a call is fifteen percent who wanted to go; _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s subscription that switches off a buyer's price-shopping radar (Chapter 8, pp. 246–248), because ADR-0042 P1 chose the charge that happens only when work does; _Entrepreneur Revolution_'s offer that cannot be price-compared (Chapter 5, PDF pp. 118–120), as in the first position; never asking on the screen, because a Practice who leaves in silence has told the product nothing and cannot be asked later without chasing; an email after deletion, because a message on her leaving is the chase ADR-0043 P4 forbids.

### The feature list follows from the Engagement and from nothing else

A feature ships when a Practice cannot run one Engagement from intake to care ended using only Doula Cloud without it, which is #172's second test kept as the standing rule after launch; never because a competitor has it. "One place" is no build goal and no claim: the claim half was decided as form, since ADR-0047 P4 allows the site no adjective about itself and [#994](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/994)'s rule keeps the interviewer from offering the words. A pilot Practice's ask is evidence (ADR-0047 P4) and becomes work when more than one Practice asks for the same thing or her act on the product shows the Engagement cannot be run without it, which is Blank's "not custom at all" read through ADR-0046's counts. There is no "better by" number: the product's better-by is the shape, paid when she is, measured by nothing but bought again (ADR-0046 P2). On this rule [#1093](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1093) is built because an agency cannot run a birth without knowing who is on call, with Doulado's windows as what it was checked against and not the reason. The table stakes on #173 are not re-ruled: #172's replacement test decided them, and every one that shipped, shipped on it. The founder's reason, in his words: "sure"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that a claim everyone makes holds nobody.

Weighed for: _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s customer brief that ends with the minimum feature set, the smallest or least complicated problem the customer will pay to have solved, and its rule that if enough customers ask for the same custom features "they are not custom at all" ([the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany](../research/books/the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany.md), Chapter 3, pp. 157–158; Chapter 4, p. 310); _Forget the Funnel_'s one Job, since trying to solve every Job at once is one of the biggest mistakes its authors see, and its map from what customers said was valuable to the features that created it (ch. 5, PDF p. 66; ch. 7, PDF pp. 98–101); _Competing Against Luck_'s surface growth, doing many jobs for many customers, which leaves a company exposed to a rival focused on one job, and its products that succeed on the experiences they enable rather than the features they carry (ch. 8, PDF pp. 166–167; ch. 6, PDF p. 116); _Software as a Science_'s Hiten Shah, that without a single killer core use case product-market fit is a dream diluted by features, and its bundling of unwanted features into the top plan as harming retention (ch. 6, PDF p. 126; ch. 9, PDF pp. 205–206); _The Lean Startup_'s most people who overestimate how many features are needed ([the-lean-startup](../research/books/the-lean-startup.md), Ch. 6 Test, PDF pp. 95, 97); _This Is Marketing_'s marketing that is not a race to add features for less money (Ch. 3, PDF p. 26); _Company of One_'s Casper making three mattresses rather than 108 (ch. 1, PDF pp. 25–27); _Jobs To Be Done_'s feature comparison as a waste and its table stakes, outcomes very important and very satisfied by existing products (ch. 4 §VI, pp. 103, 105); _Start Small, Stay Small_'s product that still "has to be good" (Ch. 1, p. 5; Ch. 2, p. 40), honored by the replacement test; _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s features at first irrelevant in a new market (Chapter 2, pp. 90–95). Rejected: _Jobs To Be Done_'s entire job on one platform (ch. 4 §X, p. 122) and its switching threshold of upwards of 20 percent better with under 5 percent enticing nobody (ch. 3, p. 78; ch. 2, pp. 48–49); _Zero to One_'s ten times better (ch. 5, PDF p. 38); _Entrepreneur Revolution_'s OOPS, only one product or service, as the first of four small-business failures (Chapter 5, PDF pp. 108–114), because one product with one price is ADR-0042 P4's decision and a second product is [#809](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/809)'s question; _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s no new features until the market search is exhausted (Chapter 3, p. 203), because the pilot is the market search and a Practice on real Clients is the exhausting of it; _Start Small, Stay Small_'s mediocre product with brilliant marketing and execution that will make money (Ch. 3, p. 91), because a Client's records are not the place to be mediocre.

### Competition is named, first is no reason, a copier is welcomed, and the repository stays public

Competition is named and never escaped. The founder says who else sells to doulas when asked and never claims there is nobody; no surface says "the only", "the first" or "unlike every other tool". Being first to price per Engagement is stated nowhere as a reason to choose the product; what is held is the model over time, measured by bought again. A competitor who prices per Engagement is welcomed as the model proven; what remains is the refusals, which a subscription business copying the price does not thereby copy, and if it copies those too the doula has two good choices. Nothing is kept secret to slow a copy: the repository stays public, and the pilot's privacy is the Practice's own ([#421](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/421), [#444](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/444)), not a wall around the product. The private option is priced as a fact on [#1398](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1398); no reason to go private is on record, and #1398's last acceptance criterion records the reason if one appears, since a privacy reason leaves this position standing and a copy reason reverses it. The category, the competitive alternative and the primary buyer are [#999](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/999)'s in November and were not touched (ADR-0044 P3). The founder's reason, in his words: "repo can stay public, but I think we should price out the private repo option I think GHA minutes is the only thing that would cost, file a ticket to consider the cost", and "stay public".

Weighed for: _Zero to One_'s understating the scale of competition as the biggest mistake a startup can make, its moving first as a tactic rather than a goal, and its monopoly that is only valuable if it endures, measured by future cash flow (ch. 3, PDF pp. 23, 25; ch. 5, PDF pp. 44–45; ch. 5, PDF p. 35); _Lean Marketing_'s claim of having no competitors as "a huge red flag indicating low demand" (Chapter 3, p. 54); _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s rule that in a new market it is tempting to claim there is no competition and wrong to do so (Web/Mobile, Ch. 3, PDF p. 299); _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s first-mover advantage as a fallacy, on Golder and Tellis's 1993 study of 500 brands in 50 categories in which almost half of market pioneers failed while early market leaders entering an average of thirteen years later failed at 8 percent (Chapter 5, pp. 377–379); _This Is Marketing_'s spot on the map with edges rather than a cornered competitor (Ch. 5, PDF pp. 45, 54-55), held as the shape #999 will draw; _Competing Against Luck_'s Toyota, which opened its plants to rival executives twice a month and was not matched (ch. 7, PDF pp. 140–142); _Start Small, Stay Small_'s shortened autopilot (Ch. 7, p. 192), accepted as the consequence; ADR-0047 P3's consent to the notes file, asked on the basis that the repository is public, which a private repository would not weaken and does not need. Rejected: _Zero to One_'s monopoly as the goal, "capitalism and competition are opposites" and monopoly as "the condition of every successful business" (ch. 3, PDF pp. 21, 28), because a doula with one choice is the buyer every subscription tool already has and the product exists to be the other choice; _Make_'s first in a niche as a moat (Idea, pp. 25–28); _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s completely open site that invites competitors and its invite-only site to control access while the solution is tested (Web/Mobile, Ch. 5, PDF p. 420), because the pilot is private for the Practice's sake already, the code has been public for the whole build, and going private now would not take back what is already read; _Lean Marketing_'s competitor who cannot simply copy a markup (Chapter 3, p. 71), because it can, and the position does not depend on its not being.

### First Value is her first Client's Engagement existing

First Value is her first Client's Engagement existing. The empty Practice asks for one act, add a Client and start her Engagement, and asks for nothing else; it is reachable within three acts of signup, sign up, add a Client, start the Engagement, and the build ticket verifies that count on trunk rather than this ADR asserting it. Value Realization is that Engagement reaching care ended on the product, which is the spent Credit ADR-0046 P2 already counts, so no new measure is needed and none is added. No tour. No checklist that ticks features off as she touches them, because a list of what she has not yet done is the product reading her use back to her, which is ADR-0046 P5's line seen from inside the app; an empty state is her own data shown to her, not a measure. No time in the app is measured. The fourteen-day message on [#1381](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1381) is the one nudge (ADR-0043 P3) and there is no series (ADR-0043 P2). The founder's reason, in his words: "yes"; the position rests on the reasoning put to him, that the one act is the Client she already has and not a template to configure.

Weighed for: _Software as a Science_'s First Value as the one moment that makes a user understand the product, and its Rule of Three that caps the steps between signup and it at three (ch. 6, PDF pp. 126, 137, 139); _Forget the Funnel_'s failure mode at the first screen, throwing every feature at a new customer instead of the one that brought them in, and its Value Realization as the best indicator that a customer will pay (ch. 6, PDF p. 74; ch. 8, PDF p. 112; ch. 7, PDF pp. 101–102); _Lean Marketing_'s thirty-day trial never set up because the data import looked daunting (Chapter 14, pp. 231–232); _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s retention planned before acquisition succeeds (Web/Mobile, Ch. 3, PDF p. 341); _The Mom Test_'s trial that costs the person ten seconds and carries nothing until its price is raised ([the-mom-test](../research/books/the-mom-test.md), ch. 5, p. 70), honored because the act costs her a real family; _Competing Against Luck_'s habits of the present that a new hire must fire (ch. 5, PDF pp. 93–95); _The Best Interface Is No Interface_'s argument against screen-time engagement ([the-best-interface-is-no-interface](../research/books/the-best-interface-is-no-interface.md), ch. 5); ADR-0018's reason, the empty filing cabinet as the evaluator's abandon point. Rejected: _Forget the Funnel_'s in-app checklist and onboarding email series that doubled SparkToro's free-to-paid conversions in two months (ch. 8, PDF pp. 110–111), the series on ADR-0043 and the checklist on ADR-0046 P5; a tour of the features; _Obviously Awesome_'s retention attributes excluded from the consideration list (Step 5, pp. 82–83), because it is a positioning rule and belongs to #999.

### The contractor's login across Practices is not leaned on

One person is one Staff record however many Practices she works at, a Membership per Practice, and an Offer may carry an Invitation, so the model already lets a contractor Doula keep one login at every agency she works for. The recommendation named that as the one network the model has; the founder corrected the premise with a fact about how the work is done, which ADR-0045 P5 counts as evidence: a contractor Doula working for several agencies is not common. So it is not named as a network, it stays in the model because an Offer that carries an Invitation needs it, and nothing is built to grow it: no browsable directory, no "find a doula", no signal to a Practice that a person is already on Doula Cloud beyond what her accepting the Invitation shows, and no Credit for an introduction (ADR-0047 P6). How common it is becomes a past-tense question on #994's guide for the agency owner. Whether a person ever gets her own view across her Practices is a feature question decided on the fourth position's rule by the ticket that owns it. The founder's reason, in his words: "probably not that common of a thing actually".

Weighed for: _The Lean Startup_'s one rule of sustainable growth, that new customers come from the actions of past customers (Ch. 10 Grow, PDF p. 200), which is still how a second agency hears the name; _Lean Marketing_'s network effects as the strongest form of referral while "not always easy or possible to build" (Chapter 14, p. 244), the caveat being the finding here; _Start Small, Stay Small_'s vertical market whose members talk to one another and gather in the same places (Ch. 2, pp. 62–64). Rejected: _Zero to One_'s network effects, unreachable unless the product is valuable to its first users while the network is small (ch. 5, PDF pp. 38–41), as a thing to lean on, because the network is rarer than the model assumed; a directory or marketplace, because it turns an Offer addressed to a person into the job posting [#230](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/230) refused and reads a doula's use to sell her (ADR-0046 P5); a contractor-first product, because ADR-0045 P1 hears both segments before either is named primary and the contractor is neither; _Software as a Science_'s anointed advocate, least likely to churn because the status was announced publicly (ch. 8, PDF pp. 174, 177), already rejected on ADR-0047 P6.

ADR-0008, employment type gates the Practice and the Attachment gates the Engagement: held, and it is the reason the login across Practices reaches nothing by itself. #173's table stakes and gaps: held, the table stakes decided by #172's replacement test and the gaps read on the fourth position's rule, never as differentiation for its own sake. #995's findings: held, and "one place" claimed by ten of eleven is why it is no goal. [#439](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/439) and [#45](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/45), the Engagement as the unit and no subscription: held, and the first position rests on them against _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s subscription (Chapter 8, pp. 246–248) and _Software as a Science_'s four numbers built on monthly churn and monthly ARPA (ch. 1, PDF pp. 30–31). [#390](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/390)'s refund: held, as part of the third position. ADR-0031's deletion with no call: held, and the third position adds one optional question to its screen and one line to its restore screen. [#991](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/991)'s two buyers: held, and the seventh position refuses a third. The brief's conventional pattern ([#409](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/409)): held, and the sixth position's one act is the pattern applied to the empty state. The evaluator persona's question, built for what she does and can she get out again: held, and the third position answers the second half as a commitment. #994's rule that the interviewer never offers "one place": held, and the fourth position extends it to every surface as a claim. The Connected account as Accounts v2 (ADR-0007): held, and with [ADR-0010](0010-notification-email-delivery-is-an-outbox-not-in-request.md)'s outbox and [ADR-0014](0014-the-waitlist-lives-outside-our-stack.md)'s waitlist it answers the dependency risk the books agree on, _Make_'s business built on another company's API that can be shut down at any time (Build, p. 72), _Start Small, Stay Small_'s platform vendor that may absorb a plug-in's feature (Ch. 3, pp. 108–110), _This Is Marketing_'s social platform as a sharecropper's arrangement (Ch. 17, PDF p. 153) and _Lean Marketing_'s tenant who can be evicted (Chapter 13, p. 212): Stripe is a rail and never a moat, and no integration is built to hold her.

Held from the books' agreements without a question: narrow first, honored by Rochester as the first room and bounded by ADR-0045's how-narrow section, so _Zero to One_'s err on the side of too small (ch. 5, PDF pp. 41–42), _This Is Marketing_'s smallest viable market (Ch. 4, PDF pp. 34-35), _The Mom Test_'s who-where pair (ch. 7, pp. 95, 97), _Obviously Awesome_'s Big Fish, Small Pond (Step 8, pp. 104–106) and both of Dib's "an inch wide and a mile deep" (_The 1-Page Marketing Plan_, Chapter 1, p. 52; _Lean Marketing_, Chapter 2, p. 36) are not re-decided; retention cheaper than acquisition, honored by the second and sixth positions and measured only by ADR-0046's two counts, never a rate below fifty, so _Company of One_'s new customer at five times an existing one (ch. 7, PDF p. 86) and _Software as a Science_'s monthly churn rate (ch. 1, PDF pp. 30–31) are weighed as reasons and never as figures the product reads; the thing a competitor cannot copy is not the product, which is the first position. On the evidence: _The Voltage Effect_'s randomized test of six membership prices offered to about 1.2 million riders (Chapter 2, PDF p. 52) and _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s Golder and Tellis (Chapter 5, pp. 377–379) are the two measured claims on the theme, and both stand behind the third and fifth positions; every other claim weighed above is its author's own practice or aphorism, held as experience and none as a measurement, which is _Thinking in Bets_' rule that a guessed number is stated as a guess ([thinking-in-bets](../research/books/thinking-in-bets.md), ch. 6, PDF p. 166), as ADR-0045 already recorded it.

## What was considered instead

**Switching costs built deliberately after launch, Ulwick's entire job on one platform, the founder's own person, or the domain features as the moat.** Rejected because each of Walling's four is already refused by a record, a claim ten of eleven competitors make holds nobody, a business resting on one person has List's ceiling, and a product is copyable while the model is not.

**Dib's engineered reasons to stay, or nothing beyond the model.** Rejected because a list the product may not nurture and a site with one ask leave engineered reasons no surface, and because a person answering is the one thing on the list a competitor cannot copy by adding a feature.

**Leaving free as the current state, open to switching costs later.** Rejected because a moat dug behind a woman who trusted the product with her Clients' records is the sneaking around ADR-0047 already refuses, and the evaluator's own question is whether she can get out again.

**Never asking why she is leaving, or asking by email after she has gone.** Rejected because a Practice who leaves in silence has told the product nothing and cannot be asked later without chasing, and a message on her leaving is the chase ADR-0043 forbids.

**Building toward "one place", ten times better on the on-call rota, or a freeze on every feature until the market search is exhausted.** Rejected because a claim everyone makes holds nobody, no rota is ten times anything, and the pilot is the market search.

**Thiel's monopoly as the goal, first-in-niche as the moat, or the repository closed until launch.** Rejected because a doula with one choice is the buyer every subscription tool already has, first is not held, and the code has been public for the whole build.

**Going private to slow a copy.** Not decided, because no reason to go private is on record; #1398 prices the option, and its last acceptance criterion records the reason if one appears.

**An onboarding checklist and email series, or a tour of the features.** Rejected because a list of what she has not yet done is the product reading her use back to her, the series is refused by ADR-0043, and every feature at once is the failure the books name.

**The contractor's login across Practices as the one network the product leans on, a directory, or a contractor-first product.** Rejected because the founder's own knowledge of the work says a contractor at several agencies is not common, a directory turns an Offer into a job posting, and the contractor is neither of the two segments.

## Consequences

- `docs/manifesto.md` gains one line for this ADR, and nothing else. `CONTEXT.md` is unchanged.
- "Keep" is the product's word for what it does about a Practice staying, and "hold" is refused. A ticket that proposes anything whose reason is that she cannot leave, a format only Doula Cloud reads, a throttled or gated export, a balance held against her departure, a call before deletion, or a contract term, is tested against this ADR, and a change lands as a superseding ADR, not an edit here.
- The screen where an Owner starts deletion asks one optional free-text question, "What are you moving to?", once, kept with the deletion record beside who started it and when, and read by the founder by hand; no email follows a deletion. The restore screen states that the archive is taken before deletion starts or after a restore, because the export route is not one of the window's exemptions. Both land as one build ticket filed from #1377.
- After launch, a feature ships when a Practice cannot run one Engagement from intake to care ended using only Doula Cloud without it, never because a competitor has it; a pilot Practice's ask becomes work when more than one Practice asks for the same thing or her act on the product shows it. "One place" is no goal and no claim on any surface. #1093's reason is that an agency cannot run a birth without knowing who is on call, and its body says so.
- The founder names the competition when asked and never claims there is none; no surface says "the only", "the first" or "unlike every other tool". A competitor who prices per Engagement is welcomed, and no position here changes when one does. The repository stays public; #1398 prices the private option and records the reason if one ever appears.
- The empty Practice asks for one act, add a Client and start her Engagement, and the build ticket that owns that screen verifies the act is within three of signup on trunk. No tour, no checklist, no time in the app measured.
- The founder answers `hello@doula.cloud` himself through the pilot and the first year, with no ticket system and no bot in front of him; #1378 sets the hours and what happens when he cannot answer.
- #994's guide gains one past-tense question for the agency owner: the last time one of her contractor Doulas also worked for another agency. Nothing is built to grow the login across Practices, and no directory, "find a doula" or presence signal exists.
- Where a later grilling session on another theme touches what keeps a Practice, what a competitor can copy, or what a feature is built for, this ADR is the record it tests against.
