# A doula is heard before she is chosen

Who Doula Cloud is for, what the nine personas count as, and how the product learns were decided one record at a time. `docs/personas/README.md` and the **Persona** and **Extra** entries in `CONTEXT.md`, decided on [#761](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/761), fixed the nine as proto-personas, each a hypothesis to be falsified and never to be cited as user research. [#991](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/991) named Practice owners and solo doulas as the buyers, one to be named primary by [#999](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/999), put parents and referral sources out of scope, and recorded the pilot agency as the only real customer contact. [#994](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/994) wrote the interview guide on 2026-09-08, asking about the past and never "would you", banning the price question, and recording that solo doulas outside an agency are not represented. [#243](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/243) holds the standing questions for pilot Practices under the convention that a decision blocked on one is provisional until she answers. [#759](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/759) and [#760](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/760) made the sandbox agents only and a Friction log heuristic evaluation, never user research. [#1265](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1265), a second Practice on real Clients before launch, was closed as not planned on 2026-09-11 with the line that anything in it worth doing is filed again in the form the founder decides. The pilot terms on [#444](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/444) run a private pilot with a small number of practices from October 2026, and the Google Cloud BAA on [#336](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/336) exists in the founder's own name. [ADR-0044](0044-the-founder-goes-where-doulas-already-gather-and-the-site-is-arrived-at.md) P3 holds no positioning claim in public until #999 has made one. Those records hold the mechanisms. None of them said whether the solo doula is heard before one segment is chosen, whether a second Practice shapes the product before January, what the founder's own knowledge of the work counts as, or what "heard from" means.

On 2026-09-18 the founder answered those questions against the twenty-nine books on the values track, in the fourth grilling session under [ADR-0041](0041-a-book-enters-the-repo-as-evidence-and-becomes-a-rule-only-through-a-decision.md), on [#1374](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1374), from the theme synthesis at [`docs/research/books/themes/who-it-is-for-and-how-the-product-learns.md`](../research/books/themes/who-it-is-for-and-how-the-product-learns.md). Nine questions over two rounds. Once the founder took a position against the recommendation put to him, and the record says so. He confirmed the positions record on that ticket. This ADR is the value behind those records: why each position holds, in his reasons, with the evidence weighed and what was rejected. Page citations are as the read-backs in `docs/research/books/` give them, each under its own page rule. A book is evidence here and nowhere the reason. The repository is public: of the founder this ADR says that he is not a doula and knows a great many doulas personally, and nothing more about how he knows the work.

**A doula is heard before she is chosen. The founder knows how the work is done; only a doula's own words in a call, or her act on the product, say what she buys. The nine personas stay hypotheses, and nobody is a customer until she has paid.**

## The decision

### Both segments are heard once before one is named primary

Before #999 names a primary buyer, the product hears from both segments: the agency owner and two or three of her doulas on #994's guide, and three to five solo doulas the founder already knows, solo and not the agency, on the same guide, every call done by October 31, 2026. At least one solo doula is someone he has not spoken with about the product. The notes file, `docs/research/pilot-agency-interviews.md`, gets a section per solo call and records how each interviewee was found, and the summary on #994 says what the solos said that the agency did not. #999 runs in November and [#868](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/868) is written from it in December. The sample is a Rochester sample of people the founder knows, and this ADR records that as the ceiling on what #999 may claim, written beside the primary-buyer choice as its falsifying signal. The founder's reason: yes to both segments before the choice; a primary buyer chosen between two segments with one of them never heard is a target nobody has met.

Weighed for: _The Mom Test_'s stop rule, keep talking until you stop hearing new information, which can be three to five conversations for a relatively simple industry and a focused segment ([the-mom-test](../research/books/the-mom-test.md), ch. 6, p. 89); its warning that a segment can be broad in a sneaky way, so that twenty conversations turn out to be one each with twenty kinds of customer (ch. 7, pp. 92–93); its rule that friends make acceptable interviewees as long as the questions are good, and that the warm-introduction method is the one the book is written for (ch. 4, p. 56; ch. 6, pp. 82, 85); _Jobs To Be Done_'s phantom target ([jobs-to-be-done](../research/books/jobs-to-be-done.md), ch. 2, p. 52); _Start Marketing the Day You Start Coding_'s at least two customers as the test ([start-marketing-the-day-you-start-coding](../research/books/start-marketing-the-day-you-start-coding.md), p. 88); _The Voltage Effect_'s selection bias, the people who opt in being the ones most likely to benefit, named in the notes file beside each call rather than avoided ([the-voltage-effect](../research/books/the-voltage-effect.md), Chapter 2, PDF pp. 55, 57–58); _Competing Against Luck_'s many data points on a small number of people ([competing-against-luck](../research/books/competing-against-luck.md), ch. 5, PDF p. 99), which is what a twenty-minute call on one person's last client is. Held: _Thinking in Bets_' rule that the asker withholds his own conclusion ([thinking-in-bets](../research/books/thinking-in-bets.md), ch. 5, PDF p. 134), which #994's rules already enforce by keeping the product out of the room.

Deferred to a second loop after the pilot: _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s ten or twenty customers off a list of fifty names, and its several markets and users tried rather than one ([the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany](../research/books/the-four-steps-to-the-epiphany.md), Chapter 3, pp. 180, 224, 230); _Forget the Funnel_'s 10 to 15 interviews ([forget-the-funnel](../research/books/forget-the-funnel.md), ch. 3, PDF pp. 43–44). Rejected: _Entrepreneur Revolution_'s 150 surveyed and at least 30 interviewed ([entrepreneur-revolution](../research/books/entrepreneur-revolution.md), Chapter 4, PDF p. 99) and _Jobs To Be Done_'s segmentation survey of 180 to 3,000 (ch. 4 §IV, p. 98), because [ADR-0043](0043-a-date-is-a-trigger-only-when-someone-set-it.md) P2 closes the waitlist until January, Doula Office Hours' own rule forbids the post, and a pre-launch company has no 180 customers; _Jobs To Be Done_'s advice that a small one-product company hire the author's firm (ch. 7, p. 177), because the asking is the founder's own job, which #994 already hands to him, on _The Mom Test_'s rule that customer learning is not outsourced until there is a working business model (ch. 8, p. 104) and _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s rule that getting out of the building is driven by the founders ([the-startup-owners-manual](../research/books/the-startup-owners-manual.md), Strategy Guide, Ch. 4, PDF p. 97); cold calls through the Rochester Doula Cooperative, a week per call for no better sample.

### A solo Practice is on real Clients before January

A solo Practice, different in shape from the fourteen-doula agency, agrees to the pilot terms and enters a real Client and a real Engagement by December 1, 2026. #1265 is refiled in this form as [#1390](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/1390), with #1265's own acceptance criteria: agreed to the terms, one Client and one Engagement entered, what she asked for that the agency did not recorded, and #991's customer-input note moved from one real customer contact to two. She comes by either route, first to say yes: a doula the founder knows, offered the pilot terms by hand after her interview questions are done and never before, or whoever asks first through Rochester or the DONA booth (ADR-0044 P1, P2). A real Client on the product before the LLC exists is not gated: the pilot terms already start the pilot in October and the BAA exists in the founder's name. The founder's reason: the contrast is the signal, since what a solo doula has that the agency does not is nothing, no roster, no Admin, no contractors; a second agency repeats the sample; and a Practice whose first Engagement is on the last day of December has taught the product nothing before the site opens.

Weighed for: _Start Marketing the Day You Start Coding_'s test that working directly with at least two customers is how a founder knows the features add value (p. 88); _The Lean Startup_'s customers inside the sandbox treated as real, with a long-term relationship allowed ([the-lean-startup](../research/books/the-lean-startup.md), Ch. 12 Innovate, PDF p. 252); _The Voltage Effect_'s answer to selection bias, variation in the pilot population rather than a bigger pilot, the Nurse-Family Partnership piloted in three cities (Chapter 2, PDF pp. 59–60); _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_'s several markets and users rather than one (Chapter 3, p. 230); _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s earlyvangelist, the buyer who has the problem, knows it, and has cobbled together a fix, and its strong-enough signal from five of the first twelve customers called on (Strategy Guide, Ch. 4, PDF pp. 99–102; Strategy Guide, Ch. 3, PDF pp. 79–80); _The Mom Test_'s rule that a free thing counts as commitment only in proportion to what it costs the person (ch. 5, p. 70), with [ADR-0042](0042-a-practice-pays-per-engagement-and-doula-cloud-is-paid-when-the-doula-is.md)'s finding that a Credit spent on a real family is not cheap. Answered rather than rejected: _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s few high-value customers where velocity is not the goal (Web/Mobile, Ch. 5, PDF p. 420), since one Practice is few; _Zero to One_'s PalmPilot, a scattered population nobody needed the product for ([zero-to-one](../research/books/zero-to-one.md), ch. 5, PDF p. 42), since Rochester is one room. Rejected: no second Practice before January with the one-agency ceiling recorded as a risk; a second agency.

### The standing questions are asked about the last time, and the price only after the grant runs out

Every "would you" on #243 is rewritten to "tell me about the last time" before it is asked, and the price question is asked only of a Practice whose founding grant has run out, as what she did when the three ran out. The questions keep their substance; the shape changes, and the rewritten forms are a comment on #243. The founder's reason: a pilot Practice's past includes every screen, so every "would you" has a "last time" behind it, and the answer to "what did you do when the three ran out" is an act, which ADR-0042's own price review in the second quarter of 2027 already reads.

Weighed for: _The Mom Test_, specifics in the past instead of opinions about the future, and only the market can say whether an idea is good (ch. 1, pp. 8, 13, 16); _Competing Against Luck_'s milk shake chain, which shipped every survey answer and saw no change in sales (ch. 1, PDF pp. 19–20; ch. 5, PDF pp. 92–93); _This Is Marketing_, people are terrible at inventing new ways to meet a want they already know ([this-is-marketing](../research/books/this-is-marketing.md), Ch. 7, PDF p. 69); _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_, surveys are answered with logic and buying is done with emotion, so asking is supplemented with observing ([the-1-page-marketing-plan](../research/books/the-1-page-marketing-plan.md), Chapter 2, p. 87); _The Lean Startup_, customers often do not know what they want (Ch. 9 Batch, PDF p. 195); _Forget the Funnel_, customers are not product experts (ch. 3, PDF p. 41); _Thinking in Bets_, the asker withholds his own conclusion (ch. 5, PDF p. 134). Kept: _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s customer with a genuine complaint as an intelligence asset (Chapter 8, p. 260) and _Company of One_'s figure that only 4 percent of dissatisfied customers complain ([company-of-one](../research/books/company-of-one.md), ch. 7, PDF pp. 85–86, 95), which is why the pilot's objections are read on #243 and never waited for. Rejected: #243 as written; striking only the price question.

### The loss persona gets a doula's input

Nadia Haddad, the Client whose pregnancy ends in loss, gets the input her README entry asks for through one question added last to #994's doula branch, asked with consent and with leave to skip: the last time a client's pregnancy ended before the birth, what the doula did in the days after, and what she wrote down and where. The interviewee is the doula, never the Client, so no PHI enters the repo. The answer goes in the notes file and is read on [#325](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/325); if no doula in the sample has supported a loss, the fallback is one separate conversation with a doula found through the Rochester room; [#329](https://github.com/markgoho/doula-cloud/issues/329)'s second run does not wait for either. The founder's reason: the cheapest route that is not nothing; it asks what happened, never what she would want, and a doula who has done it will say so in a minute.

Weighed for: _Design for Cognitive Bias_, the people most impacted tend to have the least input, participatory design's top rung implements what the participants decide, and design for the most vulnerable first ([design-for-cognitive-bias](../research/books/design-for-cognitive-bias.md), ch. 4, pp. 77, 80–83, 85); _Competing Against Luck_'s timeline built by asking what happened rather than what the customer would want (ch. 5, PDF pp. 99–110); the README's own line that her journey should not be finalized on assumption alone. Rejected: nothing before launch, on the ground that [ADR-0038](0038-overdue-is-derived-and-notifies-nobody.md) applied her journey and the pilot will show what is wrong; a separate conversation as the first route, since it costs a second person for the same answer.

### The founder's own knowledge is evidence of the work, never of the market

The founder is not a doula and knows a great many doulas personally. What he knows of the work is evidence of how the work is done: a position on what a visit is, what a contract says, or what an on-call night looks like may rest on it. It is never evidence of the market: a position on what she pays for today, which of the two segments buys, or what words she searches rests on a call recorded in `docs/research/pilot-agency-interviews.md` or on a Practice's act on the product, and on nothing else. The nine personas keep their standing as hypotheses, and `docs/personas/README.md` now says they are built from the founder's own knowledge of the work and from the code, which is not research, so the source is named honestly and the standing does not change. The founder's reason, in his words: he knows a great many doulas personally; he has lived beside the work for years and heard it described without the product in the room, and the personas were built from that knowledge and nothing in the pilot has contradicted one. The recommendation put to him, that his knowledge is a hypothesis of the same standing as a proto-persona, was not taken; the line between the work and the market was put after that and taken.

Weighed for: _Make_, ideas come from solving your own problems, the founder is the greatest expert at them, and talking to customers to find problems is an outside perspective ([make-the-bootstrappers-handbook](../research/books/make-the-bootstrappers-handbook.md), Idea, pp. 20–23); _Start Small, Stay Small_'s warm niche, one the founder already has an association with, against a cold one entered without an insider, the chances of which the author calls dubious at best ([start-small-stay-small](../research/books/start-small-stay-small.md), Ch. 2, pp. 45–51, 62–65); _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s observing as the supplement to asking (Chapter 2, p. 87); _Competing Against Luck_'s rule that personas, ethnography and the rest stay valid as a starting point under a different lens, and that a need is not a job until the circumstance is known (ch. 2, PDF pp. 39–40; ch. 4, PDF pp. 73–74).

Weighed against and answered by the line rather than rejected, since each is about the market half and the market half rests on calls and acts: _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s first deadly sin, the assumption that the founders know what the customer wants (Strategy Guide, Ch. 1, PDF pp. 41–53); _The Voltage Effect_'s finding that a perfect customer cannot be imagined (Chapter 3, PDF p. 83); _Forget the Funnel_'s product team that believes it is its own customer, treated as an objection to answer (ch. 3, PDF p. 39); _The Lean Startup_'s facts that exist only outside the building (Ch. 5 Leap, PDF pp. 89–91). Rejected for the work half only: _Jobs To Be Done_'s rule that segments come from statistically valid research and not from observation (ch. 2, p. 52; ch. 4 §IV, pp. 95–96), since the pilot has not contradicted a persona. Rejected as instruments for the market half: _Start Small, Stay Small_'s customer profile imagined from the ideal customer's life, arrival, frustrations and jargon (Ch. 4, pp. 119–120), and _The 1-Page Marketing Plan_'s avatar (Chapter 1, pp. 59–62).

### Three counts, and only the third is a customer

What the product has "heard from" is three counts kept apart and each named when cited: people interviewed, recorded in `docs/research/pilot-agency-interviews.md`; Practices on real Clients under the pilot terms; and Practices who have bought a Credit, which is the only count the word "customer" is used for. On 2026-09-18 all three are zero: no call has been held, the pilot starts in October, and no Credit has been sold. No document says "our customers" before a Credit is bought, and #868's proof of founding facts only (ADR-0044 P6) already fits. This position rests on the records and was recorded on #1374 rather than asked; the founder confirmed it with the rest.

Weighed for: _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_, the word customer is reserved for people who pay, alpha and beta testers are not customers, and giveaways do not prove customers will buy (Chapter 4, pp. 299–301); _This Is Marketing_'s traction test, who would miss it if it were gone (Ch. 8, PDF pp. 79–80); _The Lean Startup_'s customer archetype as a hypothesis until validated learning shows the type can be served sustainably (Ch. 5 Leap, PDF pp. 92–93).

### What the record had already settled, held as recorded

The persona as an instrument: a proto-persona is a hypothesis, never user research, the nine are fixed, and a contradicted persona is wrong rather than the finding (`docs/personas/README.md`; #761). That is _The Lean Startup_'s archetype as hypothesis (Ch. 5 Leap, PDF pp. 92–93) and _The Startup Owner's Manual_'s archetype built from what customers say and kept updated (Web/Mobile, Ch. 3, PDF pp. 258–259), against _Jobs To Be Done_'s phantom target (ch. 2, p. 52), which the position above answers for the work half, and with _This Is Marketing_'s personas as the shortcut for choosing people by what they dream and believe (Ch. 4, PDF pp. 33–34), which the README's "distinct way of arriving" already is.

How narrow: two segments, Practice owners and solo doulas, with parents and referral sources out (#991); every non-US jurisdiction out ([ADR-0027](0027-erasure-redacts-in-place-and-shreds-the-key.md)); any US doula, reached through a room in Rochester first and a national community second (ADR-0044 P2). _This Is Marketing_'s smallest viable market (Ch. 4, PDF pp. 34–35) and _Zero to One_'s small concentrated group (ch. 5, PDF pp. 41–42) are honored by Rochester as the first room; _Obviously Awesome_'s floor, narrowness bounded by the near-term sales objective ([obviously-awesome](../research/books/obviously-awesome.md), Step 7, p. 90; Step 8, p. 106), and _The Voltage Effect_'s ceiling on what a narrow pilot represents (Chapter 2, PDF pp. 55, 57–58) are honored by the national channel and by the recorded ceiling above; _Lean Marketing_'s five questions on whether two audiences are one market or two ([lean-marketing](../research/books/lean-marketing.md), Chapter 2, p. 39) are #999's to answer.

What an interview may change: a provisional decision on #243 is confirmed or reversed by a pilot Practice's answer, and a vocabulary mismatch on #994 is evidence about the outward-facing words, never about `CONTEXT.md`. _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_ holds the same line, that the first product specification comes from the founders' vision and Customer Discovery is neither a feature list from prospects nor a focus group (Chapter 3, pp. 79–80, 111). Who asks: the founder, on #994 and on the widest agreement on the track.

On the evidence: _The Voltage Effect_'s randomized field experiments are the one measured claim on the track, and they speak to selection bias, which the first two positions accept. Every count in the books' "how many" conflict is its author's own practice, weighed above as experience and none as a measurement, which is _Thinking in Bets_' rule that a guessed number is stated as a guess (ch. 6, PDF p. 166).

## What was considered instead

**#999 on the agency alone, with the solo segment recorded as unheard.** Rejected because a primary buyer chosen with one segment never heard is a target nobody has met, and three to five calls to people the founder already knows cost less than the wrong choice.

**Blank's ten or twenty, or a survey of the list or a group.** Deferred and rejected respectively: the larger loop is for after the pilot has produced its earlyvangelists, and the list may not be written to until January.

**No second Practice before January, or a second agency.** Rejected because the contrast is the signal and a second agency repeats the sample.

**A real Engagement by December 31.** Rejected because a first Engagement on the last day of December teaches nothing before the site opens.

**#243 as written, or striking only the price question.** Rejected because every "would you" has a "last time" behind it once she is on the product, and the price is read as an act.

**Nothing for the loss persona before launch, or a separate conversation first.** Rejected because one question on a call already scheduled is the cheapest route that is not nothing.

**The founder's knowledge as a hypothesis of the same standing as a proto-persona.** Put to the founder and not taken: he knows a great many doulas personally and the pilot has not contradicted a persona. The line between the work and the market was put after that and taken.

**The founder's knowledge as evidence for the market too.** Rejected because what she pays for, which segment buys, and the words she types are the facts the calls exist to find, and Blank and Dorf's first sin is exactly the founder answering them himself.
